## 3. Technical Summary & Engineering Contracts

### 3.1 Purpose & Functional Role
Rigidly anchors the overhead inverted solar cone collector and specular pyramid deflector array to the 2020 structural extrusion frame. Off-the-shelf L-brackets cannot withstand localized thermal expansion under concentrated 1,200 W/m² photon flux without warping, and lack integrated ±15° elevation micro-adjustment slots necessary for precision optical calibration.

### 3.2 Mating Interfaces & Tolerances
Mates with standard 2020 T-slot aluminum extrusions via dual drop-in M5 steel T-nuts (20.0 mm pitch center-to-center, tolerance ±0.08 mm). The upper flange features a 3/8"-16 UNC continuous steel core anchor stud clamp for primary suspension, and four M4 counterbored clearance holes on a 50.0 mm bolt circle for collector cone coupling.

### 3.3 Fabrication & Post-Processing Instructions
CNC milled from 6061-T6 billet or 5 mm laser-cut plate with precision 90° air-brake bending. Tap all M5 and M4 mounting holes to ISO metric standards (6H thread tolerance). Deburr all edges with a 0.5 mm 45° chamfer. Apply Mil-A-8625 Type II clear anodizing for corrosion and humidity resistance in continuous 85% RH biosphere environments.
