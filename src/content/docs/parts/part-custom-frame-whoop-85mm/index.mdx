## 3. Technical Summary & Engineering Contracts

### 3.1 Purpose & Functional Role
The 85mm Whoop Airframe serves as the primary structural chassis for the **Aerial Spotter & Pollination Drone** (`aerial-pollination-drone`, Stage 1 Electromechanical Pack). In indoor agricultural facilities and vertical grow racks, conventional exposed-propeller drones immediately snag on tomato vines, vertical duckweed hydroponic towers, and support cabling. The integrated 360° ducted aerodynamic rings completely shield 2-inch propellers, enabling the micro-UAV to bump non-destructively against vegetation while performing targeted sonic flower pollination and multi-spectral canopy health checks.

### 3.2 Mating Interfaces & Tolerances
* **Central Avionics Bay:** 26×26mm standard whoop AIO flight controller mount pattern (M2 soft-mount grommets) for `PART-ELEC-FC-STM32F4-AIO`.
* **Motor Mount Pods:** 4× 3-hole M1.4 bolt circle patterns centered within each duct for 1103 10,000KV brushless outrunners (`PART-ELEC-MTR-BLDC-1103`) driving 2.0-inch (51mm) multi-blade micro propellers.
* **Battery Cradle:** Integrated underslung dual-rail snap retention cradle accommodating 2S 450mAh high-discharge LiPo battery packs (`PART-ELEC-BAT-LIPO-2S450`).
* **Acoustic Transducer Mount:** Forward payload dock securing the 27mm Piezo Acoustic Vibration Transducer (`PART-ELEC-PZO-BUZZ-27MM`) tuned to 310 Hz buzz-pollination frequency.

### 3.3 Fabrication & Post-Processing Instructions
Fabricate via FDM 3D printing in impact-resistant Polycarbonate (PC) or Food-Grade PETG (or utilize injection-molded BetaFPV Meteor85 replacement frames). Print with 0.4mm nozzle, 4 perimeters on duct rings, and 100% infill on structural motor spar arms for maximum crash durability. Deburr all edges and clean with deionized water prior to cleanroom biosafety deployment.
