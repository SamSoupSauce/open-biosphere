## 1. Component Overview

High-durability 8-pin spring-loaded gold pogo interface with magnetic kinematic self-aligning latches for toolhead swap-out. Features a 55 mm × 45 mm × 20 mm solid housing with dual kinematic alignment guide pins, dual neodymium magnetic latch retention cups, and an 8-contact (2×4 grid, 2.54 mm pitch) spring-loaded gold pogo pin array for hot-swappable gantry toolhead payload exchange.
