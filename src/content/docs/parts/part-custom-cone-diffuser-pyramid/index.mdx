## 3. Technical Summary & Engineering Contracts

### 3.1 Purpose & Functional Role
Custom fabricated optical component engineered for the Universal Solar Roof (RFC-0009) and TPS Root Chamber down-core light distribution. Collimated natural sunlight cascading down the central vertical tower strikes the four specular 45-degree facets and is reflected omnidirectionally outward onto the ceiling dome. The hollow shell geometry and open apex aperture accommodate the recessed 50W LED puck for supplemental PAR photoperiod extension without obstructing airflow or adding top-heavy cantilever mass.

### 3.2 Mating Interfaces & Tolerances
Mates with `PART-FAB-BRACKET-SOLAR-CONE` via an open base perimeter mounting flange (180.0 mm × 180.0 mm outer perimeter, 10.0 mm skirt height, tolerance ±0.1 mm). The open internal cavity provides continuous clearance for LED power wiring and passive heat sink ventilation.

### 3.3 Fabrication & Post-Processing Instructions
CNC milled or stamped 6061-T6 aluminum with specular optical mirror polish (surface roughness Ra < 0.05 µm). Ultrasonic cleaning in deionized water followed by optical-grade vapor degreasing.
