## 3. Technical Summary & Engineering Contracts

### 3.1 Purpose & Functional Role
Consolidates four distinct chemical dosing channels into a single turbulent mixing vortex prior to injection into the main duckweed and aeroponic supply loops. Off-the-shelf industrial manifolds cause channel cross-siphonage during non-actuated cycles and lack the non-metallic or food-passivated surfaces necessary to prevent nutrient oxidation.

### 3.2 Mating Interfaces & Tolerances
Four independent 1/4"-18 NPT female inlet ports spaced at 22.0 mm intervals, counter-drilled for 1/4" compression tube fittings. Single coaxial 1/2"-14 NPT central delivery discharge port. Two M4 counterbored through-holes on 80.0 mm centers for rigid mounting to 2020 frame struts.

### 3.3 Fabrication & Post-Processing Instructions
Machined from solid 6061-T6 aluminum block or virgin POM Delrin. Cross-drilled port passages reamed to 6.35 mm diameter with micro-peck cycle to ensure burr-free fluid passages. Precision tap all NPT threads with taper reamer prep. Passivate aluminum blocks with food-safe electroless nickel plating (ENP 25 μm).
