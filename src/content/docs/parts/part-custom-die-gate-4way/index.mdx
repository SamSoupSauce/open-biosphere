## 1. Component Overview & Architectural Role

Overlapping tool steel die gate plates actuated by independent stepper lead screws for dynamic continuous container width and depth adjustment.

### 4.1 Fabrication & Slicing Notes
1. **FDM Rapid Prototyping:**
   - Layer height: `0.20 mm` (First layer: `0.24 mm`).
   - Walls/Perimeters: 4 perimeters minimum for structural rigidity under extruder compression.
   - Infill: 50% gyroid infill for isotropic resistance to shear during aperture adjustment.
   - Bed leveling: Load `print.stl` for a pre-arranged plater batch fitting standard 220x220mm print beds (e.g. Ender-3, Prusa MK3/MK4) with all sub-parts grounded at `Z = 0.0 mm`.
2. **CNC Tool-Steel Milling (Production):**
   - Frame rails milled from O1 gauge plate; sliding surfaces Blanchard ground to `0.4 µm Ra`.
   - Lead screw drive pinions turned and broached with 12mm hex interface for standard NEMA 17 stepper hollow-shaft coupling.
