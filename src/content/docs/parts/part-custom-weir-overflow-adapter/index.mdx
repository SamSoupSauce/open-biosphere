## 3. Technical Summary & Engineering Contracts

### 3.1 Purpose & Functional Role
Transitions circulating surface duckweed slurries from the rectangular 140 mm × 60 mm weir throat into the 85 mm cylindrical intake of the dewatering press. Commercial PVC pipe reducers introduce blunt 90° stagnation eddies that trigger biomass clogging, biofouling, and cavitation at the auger inlet; this custom geometry provides an optimized 15° laminar velocity ramp.

### 3.2 Mating Interfaces & Tolerances
Inlet face features a 140 mm × 60 mm rectangular perimeter flange with 6x M4 stainless through-holes (hole pitch 45 mm horizontal, 50 mm vertical) sealing against a 2 mm food-grade silicone flat gasket. Outlet transitions to an 85 mm inner-diameter slip collar with an internal 3 mm EPDM O-ring groove for clamping to the dewatering cylinder.

### 3.3 Fabrication & Post-Processing Instructions
FDM 3D-printed using certified food-contact compliant PETG. Print settings: 0.6 mm hardened steel nozzle, 0.20 mm layer height, 6 solid wall perimeters, 50% gyroid infill. Post-process with solvent wipe or food-grade polyurethane sealant on all interior wet surfaces to eliminate micro-cavity bacterial entrapment.
