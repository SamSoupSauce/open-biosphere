## 1. Component Overview & Architectural Role

Twin counter-rotating toothed crusher shafts with 2mm tooth clearance for thermal debonding and crushing of spent ceramic packaging without fine dust generation.

Manufactured from wear-resistant manganese tool steel with intermeshing tooth detents. Driven at 45-60 RPM inside a 220°C thermal jacket to break spent bio-pitch ceramic containers into uniform 1-3mm angular grog without stone pulverization.
