# Global Part Registry & Canonical BOM Catalog

The **Open-Biosphere Global Part Registry** provides a single source of truth for all mechanical, electrical, fluidic, and custom-fabricated components across the 8 autonomous machines.

---

## Component Categories

* [**Mechanical Components**](/open-biosphere/parts/mechanical/): Extrusions, structural rails, bearings, drive belts, shafts, and optical mirrors.
* [**Electrical & Compute**](/open-biosphere/parts/electrical/): Microcontrollers, drivers, sensors, cameras, servos, and power supplies.
* [**Fluidic & Pneumatics**](/open-biosphere/parts/fluidic/): Pumps, valves, misting nozzles, wedge-wire filters, and manifolds.
* [**Custom Fabricated & 3D Printed**](/open-biosphere/parts/custom-fab/): Dedicated 3D models, PETG augers, silicone rollers, chassis plates, and pyramid deflectors.
