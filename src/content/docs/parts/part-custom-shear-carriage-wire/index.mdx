## 1. Component Overview & Architectural Role

Spring-tensioned high-tensile music wire carriage actuated along linear guide rails by metal-gear servo for cleanly slicing extruded container profiles to programmed Z-height.

6061-T6 aluminum sliding bow carriage holding 0.6mm spring steel piano wire under 150N tension. Traverses across the hot extruded mastic column at high speed, slicing the container walls to programmed depth with zero deformation of un-sintered edges.
