## 3. Technical Summary & Engineering Contracts

### 3.1 Purpose & Functional Role
Couples the 25T spline output of the 25kg-cm steering servo directly to the overflow weir gate pivot arm. Commercial RC plastic horns flex under hydrodynamic surge pressure and suffer from deadband hysteresis (+/-2.5 degrees), which corrupts the precise weir crest elevation needed for 1.5 mm thin-film duckweed skimming.

### 3.2 Mating Interfaces & Tolerances
Features a broached 25T standard servo spline hub with an integrated M2.5 grade 12.9 pinch clamp screw for zero backlash. Opposing bellcrank yoke features dual 3.0 mm H7 reamed clevis pin bores equipped with flanged oilite bronze bushings and stainless steel retaining E-clips.

### 3.3 Fabrication & Post-Processing Instructions
CNC milled from 6061-T6 aluminum or precision 3D-printed in Continuous Carbon-Fiber PETG (100% infill). Spline hub reamed and broached with 25T involute cutter; clevis ears drilled undersize and line-reamed to 3.00 mm (+0.01/-0.00 mm). Deburr all slot edges and hard-coat anodize (black, Mil-A-8625 Type III).
