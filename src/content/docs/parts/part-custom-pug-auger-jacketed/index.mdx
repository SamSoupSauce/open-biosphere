## 1. Component Overview & Architectural Role

304 stainless steel heated jacketed pug mill barrel (140°C–160°C) with internal progressive pitch auger screw for continuous high-shear mastic compounding.

Precision machined 304 stainless steel barrel with welded outer heating fluid/band jacket. Features continuous progressive pitch flighting that conveys coarse grog aggregate while injecting hot bio-pitch binder at 12-18% weight ratio into a homogeneous dough.
