## 3. Technical Summary & Engineering Contracts

### 3.1 Purpose & Functional Role
Custom fabricated component engineered specifically for the Open Biosphere autonomous duckweed cultivation and robotics infrastructure. Off-the-shelf commercial alternatives fail to meet the strict zero-plastic food-safe requirements, spatial envelope datum constraints, and continuous high-humidity (>85% RH) environmental resistance.

### 3.2 Mating Interfaces & Tolerances
Mates directly with standard 2020 T-slot aluminum extrusions via M4/M5 stainless fasteners and precision dowel pins (tolerance ±0.1mm). Interfaces with Stage 1 manual override linkages and Stage 2 autonomous actuator housings.

### 3.3 Fabrication & Post-Processing Instructions
Fabricate via Machined Aluminum + Die Spring in aluminum. Deburr all edges with a 0.5mm chamfer. Perform ultrasonic cleaning in deionized water. Apply food-grade surface passivation or sealing prior to deployment in crop contact loops.
