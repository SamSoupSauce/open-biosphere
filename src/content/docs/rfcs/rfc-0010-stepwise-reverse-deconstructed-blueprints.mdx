# RFC-0010: Stepwise Reverse-Deconstructed Blueprint Standard

* **RFC ID:** `RFC-0010` (Supersedes `DOCS-002`)
* **Author:** Documentation Systems, Technical CAD & Physical Assembly Engineering Team
* **Status:** `Superseded by RFC-0014 for 3D Visuals & CAD Models` *(Procedural step structure retained as canonical assembly manual standard)*
* **Target Classification:** Documentation / Technical Illustration / CAD Systems
* **Applicable Scope:** All 24 Machine Stage Manuals (`hardware/<machine-slug>/stage-{0,1,2}.mdx`)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

A single static technical blueprint illustrating only the completed machine assembly leaves builders guessing regarding:
* **Internal Fastener Access:** How to reach inner T-nuts, blind tapped holes, and retaining clips once outer panels are bolted on.
* **Component Installation Order:** Critical assembly sequences where installing part B before part A creates mechanical interference and forces full disassembly.
* **Tolerances & Clearances:** Shimming, belt tensioning, and bearing press-fit steps that must be verified before enclosing mechanisms.
* **Field Service & Reverse Troubleshooting:** When a jam occurs, a technician needs to reverse the assembly sequence layer-by-layer to isolate failing sub-components without destroying surrounding assemblies.

Standard CAD exploded views often explode parts radially into 3D space, which obscures the practical physical order of installation and hides tool clearance envelopes.

**RFC-0010** mandates that **every machine stage (`stage-0.mdx`, `stage-1.mdx`, `stage-2.mdx`) must feature a stepwise sequence of vector blueprints (`.svg`)**. The visual protocol begins with the fully integrated stage assembly and strips away exactly one discrete mechanical/electrical layer per step in reverse order (or builds upward sequentially 1:1), directly paired with the corresponding procedural instructions.

---

## 2. Structural & Architectural Directives

### 2.1 The "1:1 Blueprint-to-Step" Invariant
1. Every procedural assembly step (`### Step X.Y`) in Part 5 of a stage manual must have a dedicated, matching visual blueprint:
   `/open-biosphere/hardware/<machine-slug>/assets/stage-<N>/step-<Y>-<slug>.svg`.
2. Generic placeholder diagrams, duplicate overview diagrams, or text-only assembly steps are strictly prohibited.
3. If an assembly procedure has 5 sequential steps, it must contain exactly 5 distinct stepwise vector diagrams plus the master stage elevation overview.

### 2.2 Visual Deconstruction Order & Orientation
* **Persistent Orientation Anchor:** All stepwise SVGs for a given machine must preserve an identical viewing perspective (standard isometric, side elevation, or cross-section) and reference the canonical spatial datum ($Z=0.00$ at coping baseline).
* **Stepwise Delta Highlighting:**
  * **Active Step Components:** The specific parts being installed, bolted, or torqued in the current step must be rendered in high-contrast accent stroke (`#38bdf8`, `#0ea5e9`) with solid fills and vivid callout pointers.
  * **Foundation Context Layers:** Previously installed structural foundations must be rendered in muted, low-contrast hairline outlines (`#334155`, `#1e293b`) with dashed lines to show internal positioning.
  * **Future Unassembled Layers:** Components belonging to subsequent steps must not appear until their respective step is reached.
* **Reverse Inspection Utility:** The sequence allows a builder to scroll in reverse to troubleshoot jams, locate internal fasteners, or plan retrofits without destructive disassembly.

## 3. Directory & Asset Hierarchy

Assets are organized symmetrically in both the documentation source tree and the static public assets tree:

```text
src/content/docs/hardware/<machine-slug>/
├── stage-0.mdx
├── stage-1.mdx
├── stage-2.mdx
└── assets/
    ├── stage-0/
    │   ├── master-overview.svg
    │   ├── step-1-<name>.svg
    │   ├── step-2-<name>.svg
    │   ├── step-3-<name>.svg
    │   ├── step-4-<name>.svg
    │   └── step-5-<name>.svg
    ├── stage-1/
    │   ├── master-overview.svg
    │   └── step-*.svg
    └── stage-2/
        ├── master-overview.svg
        └── step-*.svg
```

Public distribution mirror: `public/hardware/<machine-slug>/assets/stage-<N>/step-*.svg`.

## 4. Markdown Integration Pattern

Each step block in Part 5 pairs its instructional breakdown directly with its isolated blueprint:

```markdown
### Step 1.2: Wedge-Wire Screen Installation

![Step 1.2 Mechanical Alignment](/open-biosphere/hardware/fluidic-skimmer-loop/assets/stage-0/step-2-wedge-wire-mount.svg)

1. **Alignment:** Seat the 316L wedge-wire screen (150µm slot, 300mm x 400mm) against the internal side flanges at an exact 38° decline.
2. **Gasket Seating:** Lay 3mm EPDM closed-cell rubber sealing strips along the perimeter channel to prevent frond blow-by.
3. **Clamping:** Secure the screen using four M4 thumb-screws through the clamping rails. Torque finger-tight (approx. 0.8 N·m) to prevent gasket over-compression.
```
