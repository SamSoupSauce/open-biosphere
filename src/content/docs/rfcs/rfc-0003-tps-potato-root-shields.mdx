## 1. Summary

Proposes dual-stage labyrinth light seals around aeroponic root misting bays to prevent ambient PAR light (150–250 µmol/m²/s) from penetrating root chambers during selective robotic harvesting.

---

## 2. Specification

1. **Flexible Silicone Gaskets**: Black EPDM foam baffles along gantry slide entries to maintain $< 0.05 \text{ lux}$ light levels in the root chamber.
2. **Selective Harvester Interlock**: Optical sensors verify light-seal closure before resuming ultrasonic mist cycles, completely suppressing solanine accumulation in growing mini-tubers.
