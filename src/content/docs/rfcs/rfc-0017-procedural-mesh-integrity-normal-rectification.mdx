# RFC-0017: Procedural Mesh Integrity, Normal Rectification & CSG Verification Pipeline

* **RFC ID:** `RFC-0017` (Builds upon `RFC-0014` & `RFC-0016`)
* **Author:** Sam
* **Category:** CAD Pipeline & Procedural Geometry
* **Ecosystem:** `open-biosphere` 3D Asset Standard
* **Status:** `Implemented / Canonical Standard`

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This specification establishes strict topological invariants, constructive solid geometry (CSG) verification guardrails, and an automated headless verification pipeline for procedural CAD models across the Open Biosphere catalog.

Following a comprehensive audit of procedural assets, critical geometric corruptions were identified and eliminated:
1. **Missing Features & Boolean Dropouts:** Fluidic manifolds previously generated without ports or bores due to non-manifold CSG subtractions are now fully modeled with explicit push-to-connect barb bosses, countersunk bores, and interior fluid cavities.
2. **Degenerate Normals & Mangled Polygons:** Inverted and mixed winding orders across linkages have been replaced by unified skeletal extrusions with strict counter-clockwise (CCW) exterior indices.
3. **Zero-Thickness Surfaces:** Single-walled open surfaces (such as conical funnels) are strictly prohibited for 3D print assets; all geometry must enforce a wall offset of at least 2.5 mm to create closed 2-manifold solids.
4. **Automated Headless CI Linting:** All catalog models must pass `npm run test:models` verifying bounding volume, 2-manifold edge consistency, and normal orientation.

## 5. Automated CI Linting Standard (`npm run test:models`)

Every 3D CAD asset committed to `public/assets/parts/` must satisfy the headless validation suite executed in CI:

1. **Volume Test:** Calculated bounding envelope in all 3 axes must be &gt;= 1.0 mm (eliminates 2D planes passed off as 3D stock).
2. **Manifold Check:** Every edge must connect to exactly two faces on closed fabricated solids, with zero open boundaries or non-manifold edges.
3. **Face Normal Alignment:** Centroid dot product must confirm outward-pointing face normals across convex hulls.
4. **Visual Pass:** Critical functional geometry (ports, bores, flanges, ribs) must be visually verifiable under standard directional lighting.
