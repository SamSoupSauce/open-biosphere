## 1. Summary

Specifies the drive mechanics, clamping force curves, and end-effector vacuum pods for the Mantis Branch-Crawling Robot.

---

## 2. Technical Standard

1. **Pinch Roller Material**: Cast silicone rubber (Shore 40A durometer) providing dynamic friction coefficient $\mu > 0.85$ on wet bark and stainless wire.
2. **Torque-Limited Clamping**: Current-sensed stepper actuation restricting maximum clamping force to 8.5 N to prevent branch xylem crushing.
3. **Vacuum Shearing**: Low-pressure suction funnel (15 kPa) draws ripe berries into a foam-lined deceleration pod simultaneously with micro-pedicel shearing.
