## 1. Summary

This RFC establishes the physical envelope, coordinate system, and hardware zoning standard for retrofitting a residential swimming pool (~20x10 ft footprint, ~6,000 gallons) into an automated closed-loop biosphere.

---

## 2. Motivation

Previous closed-system experiments lacked standardized spatial datums and multi-agent robotics routing, resulting in hardware collisions, uneven lighting distribution, and chaotic manual harvesting.

---

## 3. Specification

1. **Spatial Datum $(0,0,0)$**: Southwest pool coping datum for all robot localizations.
2. **Four-Tier Zoning**: Central Basin (160 sq ft), Lower Tier (40 sq ft), Mid Tier (40 sq ft), Espalier (40 sq ft).
3. **Nitrogen-Cascade Fluidic Loop**: High-EC gutter overflow cascades into the duckweed basin for bio-filtration before aeroponic sumps.
