## 1. Spatial Boundary & Dimensions

- **Surface Area**: 40 sq ft vertical canopy wall
- **Elevation**: $2.5 \text{ to } 4.0 \text{ ft}$ ($760 \text{ to } 1,220 \text{ mm}$) vertical wall span
- **Datum Coordinate**: $X = [0 \text{ to } 150 \text{ mm}]$, $Y = [0 \text{ to } 6,096 \text{ mm}]$, $Z = [760 \text{ to } 1,220 \text{ mm}]$

---

## 2. Environmental & Fluidic Baseline

| Parameter | Setpoint | Safe Operating Tolerance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ambient Air Temp** | 24.0°C | 20.0°C – 28.0°C |
| **Relative Humidity (RH)** | 65% | 55% – 70% |
| **PAR Lighting** | 400 µmol/m²/s | 350 – 500 µmol/m²/s |
| **Drip Aeroponics EC** | 1.8 mS/cm | 1.6 – 2.0 mS/cm |
| **pH** | 6.0 | 5.8 – 6.4 |

---

## 3. Resident Crops & Systems

- **Crops**: *Morus alba* (dwarf bush mulberry) and *Physalis peruviana* (goldenberry).
- **Robotics**: Mantis branch-crawling robot with soft silicone dual-opposed pinch rollers and vacuum-assisted collection pods.
