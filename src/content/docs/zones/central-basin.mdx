## 1. Spatial Boundary & Dimensions

- **Surface Area**: 160 sq ft (16.0 ft × 10.0 ft)
- **Depth**: 4.5 ft (1,372 mm) liquid depth
- **Volume**: ~5,200 Gallons recirculating nutrient solution
- **Datum Coordinate**: X = [300 to 2,748 mm], Y = [600 to 5,496 mm], Z = [-1,372 to 0 mm]

---

## 2. Environmental & Fluidic Baseline

| Parameter | Setpoint | Safe Operating Tolerance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Water Temperature** | 24.0°C | 22.0°C – 26.5°C |
| **Electrical Conductivity (EC)** | 1.4 mS/cm | 1.2 – 1.8 mS/cm |
| **pH** | 6.8 | 6.5 – 7.2 |
| **Dissolved Oxygen (DO)** | 6.5 mg/L | > 5.0 mg/L |
| **PAR Lighting** | 200 µmol/m²/s | 150 – 250 µmol/m²/s (18h photoperiod) |

---

## 3. Resident Crops & Systems

- **Primary Canopy**: *Wolffia globosa* (Mankai duckweed) floating across 70% of open water.
- **Aeroponic Sub-Surface**: True Potato Seed (TPS) mini-tubers suspended in light-tight misting sumps.
- **Hardware Integration**: Fluidic skimmer, wedge-wire dewatering run-off, moving-bed biofilters.
