## 1. Spatial Boundary & Dimensions

- **Surface Area**: 40 sq ft perimeter ribbon along the pool coping
- **Elevation**: $0.5 \text{ to } 1.2 \text{ ft}$ ($150 \text{ to } 365 \text{ mm}$) above water level
- **Datum Coordinate**: $X = [0 \text{ to } 300 \text{ mm}]$ & $[2,748 \text{ to } 3,048 \text{ mm}]$, $Y = [0 \text{ to } 6,096 \text{ mm}]$

## 3. Resident Crops & Systems

- **Crops**: *Phaseolus vulgaris* (dwarf bush snap beans) and *Vigna radiata* (mung bean dark canisters).
