## 1. Spatial Boundary & Dimensions

- **Surface Area**: 40 sq ft perimeter ribbon
- **Elevation**: $1.2 \text{ to } 2.5 \text{ ft}$ ($365 \text{ to } 760 \text{ mm}$) above water level
- **Datum Coordinate**: $X = [0 \text{ to } 450 \text{ mm}]$, $Y = [0 \text{ to } 6,096 \text{ mm}]$

---

## 2. Environmental & Fluidic Baseline

| Parameter | Setpoint | Safe Operating Tolerance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day/Night Temperature** | 25°C / 18°C | 22°C – 28°C Day / 16°C – 20°C Night |
| **Relative Humidity (RH)** | 68% | 60% – 75% RH |
| **PAR Lighting** | 450 µmol/m²/s | 300 – 550 µmol/m²/s (14–16h photoperiod) |
| **Electrical Conductivity (EC)** | 2.2 mS/cm | 1.8 – 2.4 mS/cm |
| **pH** | 6.2 | 5.8 – 6.5 |

---

## 3. Resident Crops & Systems

- **Crops**: *Portulaca oleracea* (purslane), *Capsicum annuum* (chili/sweet peppers), *Wasabia japonica* (chilled sawa troughs).
- **Robotics**: Aerial spotter pollination quadcopters and 2-axis overhead gantry linear shears.
