## Part 2: Capability Delta (Features Introduced)

* **Controller-Node-Tool (CNT) Hierarchy**: Heavy neural perception runs on the Central Biosphere Controller; the Machine Node coordinates local kinematics; tiny Edge Tools drive motors 1:1.
* **Isolated Farm AP Mesh**: Secure, air-gapped local Wi-Fi (<code>POTFRM-&lt;CLUSTER_ID&gt;</code> on <code>192.168.42.0/24</code>) with WebSocket node registration at <code>ws://192.168.42.1:8443/register</code>.
* **250ms Hardware Deadman Watchdogs**: Every Tool trips into safe, high-Z, zero-power state within 250 ms if signal is interrupted.
* **Local Zero-Cloud Edge AI**: Multi-agent perception via <code>llama-cpp-python</code> local vision models without internet dependencies.

## Part 5: Step-by-Step Commissioning, Provisioning & Autonomous Runbook

The following 5 sequential assembly steps guide the physical fabrication, mounting, and wiring of the Fluidic Skimmer & Dewatering Separator (2). Each procedural step is directly anchored by an isolated, reverse-deconstructed vector blueprint adhering to RFC-0010.

### Step 2.1: Overhead Optical Sensor & Camera Rigging

![Step 2.1 Technical Blueprint](/open-biosphere/hardware/fluidic-skimmer-loop/assets/stage-2/step-1-overhead-camera.svg)

1. **Alignment & Positioning:** MOUNT HEIGHT: Z=1400mm | FOV: 78° Over Flume
2. **Mechanical & Electrical Execution:** Sony IMX519 16MP camera mounted on 2020 overhead strut for biomass density estimation.
3. **Quality Assurance & Verification:** Inspect fastener seating, verify clearances against the technical drawing, and ensure no binding across the full range of motion.


### Step 2.2: Linear Actuator Linkage & Weir Tool Rigging

![Step 2.2 Technical Blueprint](/open-biosphere/hardware/fluidic-skimmer-loop/assets/stage-2/step-2-actuator-linkage.svg)

1. **Alignment & Positioning:** STROKE: 50.0mm | FEEDBACK: Internal Potentiometer
2. **Mechanical & Electrical Execution:** 12V micro linear actuator (50mm stroke, 64N force) coupled to floating weir gate control arm.
3. **Quality Assurance & Verification:** Inspect fastener seating, verify clearances against the technical drawing, and ensure no binding across the full range of motion.


### Step 2.3: Sump Telemetry & Ultrasonic Float Tool

![Step 2.3 Technical Blueprint](/open-biosphere/hardware/fluidic-skimmer-loop/assets/stage-2/step-3-sump-telemetry.svg)

1. **Alignment & Positioning:** RANGE: 200mm - 6000mm | OPTICAL FLOAT: Dual Prism
2. **Mechanical & Electrical Execution:** JSN-SR04T waterproof ultrasonic sensor and dual optical level probes for dry-run prevention.
3. **Quality Assurance & Verification:** Inspect fastener seating, verify clearances against the technical drawing, and ensure no binding across the full range of motion.


### Step 2.4: Skimmer Node Provisioning & BLE Tool Binding

![Step 2.4 Technical Blueprint](/open-biosphere/hardware/fluidic-skimmer-loop/assets/stage-2/step-4-node-provisioning.svg)

1. **Alignment & Positioning:** BUS: RS-485 / Native UART | PROTOCOL: CNT Standard
2. **Mechanical & Electrical Execution:** ESP32-S3 Node binding DEV_WEIR_ACT, DEV_AUGER_STEP, DEV_PUMP_VFD, and DEV_SUMP_LEVEL.
3. **Quality Assurance & Verification:** Inspect fastener seating, verify clearances against the technical drawing, and ensure no binding across the full range of motion.


### Step 2.5: 250ms Watchdog & Fail-Safe Verification

![Step 2.5 Technical Blueprint](/open-biosphere/hardware/fluidic-skimmer-loop/assets/stage-2/step-5-watchdog-verification.svg)

1. **Alignment & Positioning:** HEARTBEAT TIMEOUT: 250ms | SAFE STATE: Sump Open
2. **Mechanical & Electrical Execution:** Trip testing pump and auger shutoffs upon heartbeat loss; weir defaults to full-open overflow.
3. **Quality Assurance & Verification:** Inspect fastener seating, verify clearances against the technical drawing, and ensure no binding across the full range of motion.


### Step-by-Step Operating & Commissioning Runbook

1. **Pre-Flight Mechanical Inspection:** Verify all fasteners are torqued finger-tight or to engineering specification; inspect structural clearances against Datum Z=0.00.
2. **Electrical & Signal Continuity:** Check wiring harnesses for polarity alignment; ensure physical Master DC Switch is in the OFF position prior to power connection.
3. **Two-Control Power Sequence (RFC-0005):** Switch Master DC switch to ON; observe 0.91" status OLED initialization sequence displaying firmware build and BLE advertising state.
4. **Virtual Panel Commissioning:** Open the Open Biosphere companion app, pair via local BLE GATT, and exercise manual jog sliders through full travel envelopes.
5. **250ms Deadman Watchdog Verification:** Confirm all actuators trip to safe de-energized states immediately upon intentional signal cutoff or emergency power disconnect.
