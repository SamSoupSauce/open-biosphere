## Part 1: Stage Header & Executive Summary

> [!NOTE]
> **Stage Designation:** Stage 1 (Semi-Autonomous Electromechanical Pack)  
> **Operating Mode:** DC Motorized Continuous Duty with Ephemeral BLE Virtual Panel  
> **Power Requirement:** 12V / 24V DC Industrial Bus  
> **Physical Interlocks:** Strict Two-Control Standard (Master DC Cut + Recessed SD Factory Restore)  
> **Operator Interface:** Ephemeral Flutter Mobile Companion App over BLE GATT  

The Stage 1 electromechanical pack motorizes the **Fluidic Skimmer & Dewatering Separator**, adding continuous DC motor/pump drive governed by the **Strict Two-Control Physical Standard** (RFC-0005) and an ephemeral **Flutter Companion App Virtual Panel** over direct local BLE.

---

## Part 2: Capability Delta (Features Introduced)

* **Continuous Motorized Actuation**: Replaces human manual cranking with high-efficiency DC gearmotors, steppers, or booster pumps.
* **Strict Two-Control Physical Standard**: Eliminates physical jog buttons and potentiometer dials to prevent moisture breach and priority race conditions. Only Master DC Switch and Recessed SD Reset Button are permitted on hardware.
* **Ephemeral BLE Virtual Panel**: Jogging, speed sliders, and priming pulses execute via the Flutter companion app over direct local BLE GATT.
* **100% Preserved Manual Fallback**: Slip-clutches, bypass valves, or belt releases guarantee instant zero-power manual hand operation without disassembly.

## Part 5: Step-by-Step Retrofit, Wiring & Operating Protocol

The following 5 sequential assembly steps guide the physical fabrication, mounting, and wiring of the Fluidic Skimmer & Dewatering Separator (1). Each procedural step is directly anchored by an isolated, reverse-deconstructed vector blueprint adhering to RFC-0010.

### Step 1.1: 24V DC Magnetic Circulation Pump Installation

![Step 1.1 Technical Blueprint](/open-biosphere/hardware/fluidic-skimmer-loop/assets/stage-1/step-1-pump-plumbing.svg)

1. **Alignment & Positioning:** FLOW: 55 GPM | PRESSURE: 18 PSI | RATED: 80W
2. **Mechanical & Electrical Execution:** Brushless magnetic drive pump plumbed into lower sump bulkhead with vibration-isolation mounts.
3. **Quality Assurance & Verification:** Inspect fastener seating, verify clearances against the technical drawing, and ensure no binding across the full range of motion.


### Step 1.2: NEMA 23 Stepper & Dog Clutch Coupling

![Step 1.2 Technical Blueprint](/open-biosphere/hardware/fluidic-skimmer-loop/assets/stage-1/step-2-motor-slipclutch.svg)

1. **Alignment & Positioning:** HOLDING TORQUE: 2.8 N·m | SLIP LIMIT: 4.5 N·m
2. **Mechanical & Electrical Execution:** NEMA 23 stepper motor bracket bolted to auger chassis with spring-loaded axial slip-clutch.
3. **Quality Assurance & Verification:** Inspect fastener seating, verify clearances against the technical drawing, and ensure no binding across the full range of motion.


### Step 1.3: Strict Two-Control Enclosure Assembly

![Step 1.3 Technical Blueprint](/open-biosphere/hardware/fluidic-skimmer-loop/assets/stage-1/step-3-two-control-box.svg)

1. **Alignment & Positioning:** STANDARD: RFC-0005 | IP RATING: IP67 Weatherproof
2. **Mechanical & Electrical Execution:** Heavy-duty IP67 box with Master 24V DC switch, recessed SD reset, Micro-SD slot, and 0.91" OLED.
3. **Quality Assurance & Verification:** Inspect fastener seating, verify clearances against the technical drawing, and ensure no binding across the full range of motion.


### Step 1.4: TB6600 Driver Carrier & Power Distribution Wiring

1. **Alignment & Positioning:** SUPPLY: 24V 6.5A (150W) | MICROSTEPPING: 1/8 Step
2. **Mechanical & Electrical Execution:** Optoisolated 4.0A stepper driver, 24V 150W MeanWell power supply, and ESP32 BLE Virtual Panel.
3. **Quality Assurance & Verification:** Inspect fastener seating, verify clearances against the technical drawing, and ensure no binding across the full range of motion.


### Step 1.5: Slip-Clutch Torque Verification & Manual Override

![Step 1.5 Technical Blueprint](/open-biosphere/hardware/fluidic-skimmer-loop/assets/stage-1/step-5-torque-verification.svg)

1. **Alignment & Positioning:** DISCONNECT: Mechanical Slip | OVERRIDE: Instant
2. **Mechanical & Electrical Execution:** Verifying hand speed-crank rotates freely through slip-clutch while motor is energized.
3. **Quality Assurance & Verification:** Inspect fastener seating, verify clearances against the technical drawing, and ensure no binding across the full range of motion.


### Step-by-Step Operating & Commissioning Runbook

1. **Pre-Flight Mechanical Inspection:** Verify all fasteners are torqued finger-tight or to engineering specification; inspect structural clearances against Datum Z=0.00.
2. **Electrical & Signal Continuity:** Check wiring harnesses for polarity alignment; ensure physical Master DC Switch is in the OFF position prior to power connection.
3. **Two-Control Power Sequence (RFC-0005):** Switch Master DC switch to ON; observe 0.91" status OLED initialization sequence displaying firmware build and BLE advertising state.
4. **Virtual Panel Commissioning:** Open the Open Biosphere companion app, pair via local BLE GATT, and exercise manual jog sliders through full travel envelopes.
5. **250ms Deadman Watchdog Verification:** Confirm all actuators trip to safe de-energized states immediately upon intentional signal cutoff or emergency power disconnect.
