## 5. Autonomous Navigation & Runbook

### 5.1 Multi-Modal Tracking
1. **Perimeter Line Following:** The 5-channel magnetic sensor bar (`DEV_NAV_SNS`) reads magnetic flux from adhesive magnetic floor tape installed along the facility corridor.
2. **LiDAR SLAM & Collision Avoidance:** The 360° LiDAR continuously maps the aisle, slowing travel if obstacles or personnel are detected within 1.2 m, and halting completely at 0.4 m.
3. **Deadman Safety Interlocks:** Dual motor drivers enforce 250 ms heartbeat watchdogs from the ESP32-S3 AGV Node.

### 5.2 Step-by-Step Commissioning
1. **Chassis & Track Drive:** Mount the suspension bogies and continuous rubber tracks. Verify free rotation of bogie arms under spring tension.
2. **Powertrain & Drivers:** Install the dual 24V gearmotors and wire to the 15A smart motor driver board. Verify dog clutch freewheel disengagement.
3. **Avionics & Sensors:** Mount the ESP32-S3 AGV Node, 2D LiDAR scanner on the top mast, and the 5-channel magnetic sensor bar under the front bumper.
4. **Wireless Charging:** Fasten the 120W receiver coil to the chassis belly plate and align with floor transmitter pads at $(X=0, Y=0)$.
