## 3. Tri-Modal Control Architecture

The ground AGV operates via physical push-tiller, tethered push-button pendant, or autonomous ROS2 AI pathing:

| Control Mode | Actuation Mechanism | Operator / System Experience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Manual (Zero Power)** | Fold-out tiller handle + mechanical release pedal | Shift drive motor gearboxes into neutral with manual lever; pull or push the AGV like a cart; step on the mechanical latch pedal to eject or seat cargo canisters. |
| **Tier 2: Buttons & Pendant** | Wired/wireless industrial 3-button pendant | Press physical green/yellow buttons to drive forward or reverse; turn potentiometer knob to regulate track speed; flip toggle switch to lock/unlock docking clamps. |
| **Tier 3: Autonomous AI** | ROS2 navigation + LiDAR + magnetic guidance | Onboard computer navigates along perimeter magnetic track tape and LiDAR point clouds, docking into sprout canister bays and auto-transferring harvest totes. |

## 5. Master Biosphere Assembly Interlock

In the integrated 20x10 ft Open Biosphere installation:

1. **Perimeter Lane Routing**: Follows the magnetic guide strip embedded along the Perimeter Lower Tier ($Z = 150$ to $365$ mm above water level).
2. **Bioreactor Drum Exchange**: Automatically docks into the Revolving Sprout Bioreactor bays, unlatching ripe 4-day canisters and replacing them with freshly seeded drums.
3. **Automated Recharging**: Docks into magnetic inductive floor charge plates located at the South-West datum origin $(0,0,0)$.
