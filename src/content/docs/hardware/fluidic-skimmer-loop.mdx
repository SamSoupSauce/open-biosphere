## 1. Subsystem Architecture

The **Fluidic Skimmer & Dewatering Loop** maintains water quality and drives automated daily harvesting across the 160 sq ft Central Basin. It combines directional surface water currents with an automated wedge-wire dewatering separator to harvest *Wolffia globosa* without damaging delicate fronds.

---

## 2. Technical Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Circulation Flow Rate** | 45 – 75 Gallons per Minute (GPM) |
| **Skimmer Weir** | Variable-buoyancy bistable overflow weir (1,200 mm width) |
| **Dewatering Screen** | Stainless 316L wedge-wire screen (slot width: 150 µm) |
| **Harvest Rate** | Up to 12.5 kg wet biomass per hour |
| **Biofilter Loop** | 3-stage moving bed biofilm reactor (MBBR) + 15W UV-C clarifier |
| **Drive Pump** | Brushless DC variable-frequency magnetic-drive pump (240W) |

---

## 3. Operational Mechanics

1. **Hydraulic Sweeping**: Low-velocity submerged laminar jets create a continuous surface drift pushing floating fronds toward the southern skimmer channel.
2. **Weir Induction**: Once surface mat thickness exceeds 8 mm (detected by overhead optical line-scanners), the pneumatic weir lowers 12 mm, allowing the top water layer and *Wolffia* to cascade onto the wedge-wire screen.
3. **Centrifugal Separation**: Separated fronds fall into a dewatering auger press that reduces moisture to 82%, discharging green protein paste into sealed cold canisters.
