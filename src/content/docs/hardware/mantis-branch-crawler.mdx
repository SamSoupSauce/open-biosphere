The **Mantis Branch-Crawler Robot** is an agile, limb-climbing harvesting robot designed to navigate the rigid horizontal wire guideframes of espalier-trained fruit bushes. It can be constructed as a standalone robotic harvester for orchard trellises, vineyards, and greenhouse espaliers, or deployed along the Trellised Espalier wall of the master Open Biosphere assembly.

---

## 1. Standalone Homesteading & Off-Grid Utility

Delicate soft fruits such as mulberries, blackberries, and goldenberries bruise easily when picked with conventional robotic grippers or shaken mechanically. The Mantis crawler solves this with **soft-silicone pinch locomotion and cushioned vacuum-assisted harvesting**:

## 3. Tri-Modal Control Architecture

The crawler supports manual pole-mounted actuation, direct push-button/joystick steering, or full edge AI autonomy:

| Control Mode | Actuation Mechanism | Operator / System Experience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Manual (Zero Power)** | Mechanical Bowden cable + squeeze lever | Disengage stepper clutches and clip crawler onto trellis wire; squeeze hand lever on an extension pole to clamp silicone rollers and actuate mechanical spring shears. |
| **Tier 2: Buttons & Joystick** | Handheld RC controller / wired button pendant | Dual analog thumbsticks control forward/reverse rolling and clamp tension; pulling the index trigger fires the micro-shear and vacuum suction for on-demand picking. |
| **Tier 3: Autonomous AI** | On-device YOLOv8 vision + torque sensing | Raspberry Pi 5 / Jetson Orin processes stereo RGB-NIR feeds to identify ripe berries, calculates 3D pedicel coordinates, clamps limbs to 8.5 N max, and auto-harvests. |

## 5. Master Biosphere Assembly Interlock

In the integrated 20x10 ft Open Biosphere installation:

1. **Perimeter Wall Indexing**: Navigates the rigid stainless guideframe wires running along the North-East vertical boundary wall ($Z = 760$ to $1,220$ mm).
2. **Tethered DC Bus**: Draws continuous 24V power from an overhead slip-ring cable running along the gantry rail, eliminating battery swap downtime.
3. **Pneumatic Transfer**: Deposits harvested berries directly into the Ground Track AGV transit bins stationed on the Perimeter Lower Tier.
