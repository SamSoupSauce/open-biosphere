## 5. Autonomous Flight Runbook & Vision Interlock

### 5.1 Indoor Optical Flow Navigation
1. **Satellite-Free Localization:** Operates inside shielded bio-envelopes using the downward-facing PMW3901 optical flow sensor to integrate velocity vectors across floor canopy textures.
2. **Precision Bloom Proximity:** The forward VL53L1X time-of-flight sensor locks distance at 35 mm from target flowers, commanding the piezoelectric buzzer for a 1.5-second 310 Hz acoustic burst.
3. **Automated Perch Recovery:** When battery voltage reaches 7.0V (3.5V/cell), the flight controller enters homing mode, centering above the overhead gantry perch funnel and settling into the conical docking shoe.
