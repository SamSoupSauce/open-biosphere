## Part 2: Capability Delta (Features Introduced)

* **Controller-Node-Tool (CNT) Hierarchy**: Heavy neural perception runs on the Central Biosphere Controller; the Machine Node coordinates local kinematics; tiny Edge Tools drive motors 1:1.
* **Isolated Farm AP Mesh**: Secure, air-gapped local Wi-Fi (<code>POTFRM-&lt;CLUSTER_ID&gt;</code> on <code>192.168.42.0/24</code>) with WebSocket node registration at <code>ws://192.168.42.1:8443/register</code>.
* **250ms Hardware Deadman Watchdogs**: Every Tool trips into safe, high-Z, zero-power state within 250 ms if signal is interrupted.
* **Local Zero-Cloud Edge AI**: Multi-agent perception via <code>llama-cpp-python</code> local vision models without internet dependencies.

1. **Pre-Flight Mechanical Inspection:** Verify all fasteners are torqued finger-tight or to engineering specification; inspect structural clearances against Datum Z=0.00.
2. **Electrical & Signal Continuity:** Check wiring harnesses for polarity alignment; ensure physical Master DC Switch is in the OFF position prior to power connection.
3. **Two-Control Power Sequence (RFC-0005):** Switch Master DC switch to ON; observe 0.91" status OLED initialization sequence displaying firmware build and BLE advertising state.
4. **Virtual Panel Commissioning:** Open the Open Biosphere companion app, pair via local BLE GATT, and exercise manual jog sliders through full travel envelopes.
5. **250ms Deadman Watchdog Verification:** Confirm all actuators trip to safe de-energized states immediately upon intentional signal cutoff or emergency power disconnect.
