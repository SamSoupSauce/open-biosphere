## 1. Subsystem Overview & Baseline Operating Principles

Stage 0 establishes the foundational mechanical chassis, crushing chamber, and compaction extruder for the Bio-Pitch & Ceramic Aggregate (BCA) packaging engine. It requires **zero electrical power, zero microcontrollers, and zero automated sensors**.

The builder uses manual ratchet cranks to power the counter-rotating toothed shafts, gravity to feed coarse grog into the heated barrel, an Acme hand-wheel lead screw to tamp and extrude the warm bio-pitch mastic through the die face, and a hand-tensioned music wire bow to slice extruded container profiles to length.

## 6. Zero-Power / Manual Commissioning & Runbook Protocol

1. **Pre-Heating Binder:** Warm raw biomass pyrolysis tar in a heavy steel pot over hot coals until it reaches pouring viscosity (approx. 90°C).
2. **Breaker Operation:** Load spent ceramic shards or clean terracotta broken bisque into the top hopper. Turn the hand ratchet clockwise at 30–45 RPM. Verify that crushed aggregate drops as 1–3 mm coarse jagged grog with zero fine powder.
3. **Compounding:** Pour 15% warm bio-pitch by weight onto the aggregate in the pug barrel. Turn the auger hand crank 20 full revolutions until the mastic reaches a uniform dark, glistening dough consistency.
4. **Ram Compaction & Extrusion:** Advance the Acme lead screw hand wheel with steady continuous torque (approx. 35 N·m) to force the mastic through the die opening.
5. **Wire Cutoff:** Pull the spring bow slicer downward in a swift, single motion to cut the formed container. Transfer the rigid green vessel onto the curing plate at 180°C for 5 minutes.

## 7. Pre-Flight Verification Checklist

* [ ] Tooth-to-tooth clearance verified to $2.0 \pm 0.2$ mm across both crusher rollers.
* [ ] Acme lead-screw threads lubricated with high-temperature molybdenum disulphide paste.
* [ ] Music wire tension produces an audible high-pitch note ($>400$ Hz) and shows zero sagging under finger deflection.
* [ ] Thermal pre-heat jacket holds pressure without leaking hot gases into the operator breathing zone.
* [ ] Sample test container holds 500 mL water for 24 hours without softening or wall weeping.
