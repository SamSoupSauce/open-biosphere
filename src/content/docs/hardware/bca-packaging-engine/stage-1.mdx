## 1. Subsystem Overview & Electromechanical Principles

Stage 1 upgrades the purely manual Stage 0 baseline into a semi-automated, temperature-regulated production station. Manual hand cranks are replaced with a high-torque 12V 250W geared DC motor, and manual hearth heating is replaced with dual 300W ceramic band heaters regulated by digital PID controllers.

In accordance with [RFC-0005: Physical Hardware Interlocks & Minimalist BLE Virtual Interface Standard](/open-biosphere/rfcs/rfc-0005-hardware-interlocks-ble-virtual-panel/), physical button panels, dials, and touchscreens are strictly eliminated from the chassis. The machine exposes only the **Strict Two-Control Physical Standard**:
1. **Master Inline High-Current DC Power Disconnect:** An IP65 red rotary emergency stop cutting all 24V/12V actuator and heater circuits instantly.
2. **Factory Hardware Restore Button:** A single recessed tactile push button for clearing firmware lockouts and resetting BLE pairings.

All runtime monitoring (temperature telemetry, motor RPM, jog controls, and batch recipes) is conducted through the ephemeral **Flutter BLE Virtual Panel** running on any standard mobile phone or tablet.

## 6. Flutter BLE Virtual Panel Runbook

```text
[BCA Packaging Engine - BLE Interface]
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ STATUS: ONLINE           WATCHDOG: 182ms [OK]│
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Zone 1 (Barrel):  152.4°C  [SP: 150°C]       │
│ Zone 2 (Die):     174.8°C  [SP: 175°C]       │
│ Breaker Motor:    48 RPM   [Current: 3.4A]   │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [ JOG FWD ]    [ STOP ]    [ JOG REV (JAM) ] │
│ [ AUTO EXTRUDE BATCH ]    [ STANDBY CURE ]   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Pre-Heating Hold:** Tap **AUTO PRE-HEAT** in the mobile app. The system ramps band heaters to 150°C and maintains thermal equilibrium for 5 minutes to liquefy residual tar.
2. **Motorized Crushing:** Tap **JOG FWD** to spin the breaker shafts at 50 RPM. Pour in clean ceramic shards; observe automatic motor current telemetry (nominal 2.5A to 4.5A; over-current trip set at 12A).
3. **Automated Jam Clearing:** If foreign tramp iron enters the hopper, the H-bridge detects a current spike (&gt;10A) and automatically reverses rotation for 1.5 seconds before resuming forward drive.
4. **Thermal Watchdog:** If the mobile device disconnects for more than 250 milliseconds, the ESP32-S3 immediately disengages motor PWM and enters safe thermal hold.

## 7. Pre-Flight Verification Checklist

* [ ] Master inline DC power cutoff mechanically disconnects all actuator and heater power.
* [ ] Zero physical potentiometers, dials, or analog switches present outside the Two-Control box.
* [ ] K-type thermocouples read ambient room temperature ($22 \pm 3$°C) prior to heating.
* [ ] Roller chain tension correctly aligned with less than 10 mm slack and sprockets coplanar.
* [ ] BLE advertisement connects reliably at 10 meters distance through enclosure wall.
