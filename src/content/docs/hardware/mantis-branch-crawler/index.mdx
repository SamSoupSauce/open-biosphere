## 5. Control Architecture & Commissioning Runbook

### 5.1 Controller-Node-Tool (CNT) Standard
Mantis operates on an air-gapped farm AP network (`192.168.42.0/24`):
1. **Perception Offload:** The dual RGB-NIR cameras stream compressed RTSP H.264 video to the Central Biosphere Controller for real-time neural instance segmentation (`llama-cpp-python` GGUF vision model).
2. **Kinematic Coordination:** The ESP32-S3 Mantis Node receives target coordinates and dispatches tool commands over local high-speed serial/BLE downlinks to the 3 ESP32-C3 tool microcontrollers.
3. **Deadman Watchdogs:** Each ESP32-C3 tool enforces a 250 ms hardware timeout. If heartbeats from the node cease, motor outputs immediately float into high-impedance safe mode, and clamping levers default to mechanical slip thresholds.

### 5.2 Step-by-Step Commissioning
1. **Frame & Pinch Bogie Assembly:** Bolt the aluminum scissor plates to the 2020 spine and assemble the dual Shore 40A silicone rollers on steel shoulder bolts.
2. **Rotary Shear & Vacuum Pod:** Mount the coreless shear motor and 5015 vacuum blower onto the forward bracket; verify vacuum pull at the ripstop nylon catch funnel.
3. **Avionics & Power Rigging:** Mount the 5V 5A synchronous buck converter and wire the ESP32-S3 node controller. Connect the HX711 load cell to calibrate clamping force to 6.5 N per contact patch.
4. **Tool Provisioning:** Flash firmware on the 3 ESP32-C3 tools (`DEV_ROLLER_DRV`, `DEV_CLAMP_ACT`, `DEV_SHEAR_VAC`) and confirm 250ms watchdog trip verification.
