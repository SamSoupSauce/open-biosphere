## 5. BLE GATT Operation & Commissioning Runbook

### 5.1 BLE Service & Characteristic Map
The gantry ESP32 exposes the standard Cartesian Kinematics GATT service:
* **Service UUID:** `0000FFF0-0000-1000-8000-00805F9B34FB`
* **Target Position Vector (`0xFFF1`, Write):** `[X_target_mm: float32, Y_target_mm: float32, Feedrate_mms: float32]`
* **Current Telemetry (`0xFFF2`, Read/Notify):** `[X_current_mm: float32, Y_current_mm: float32, Limit_status: uint8]`
* **Toolhead Actuation (`0xFFF3`, Write):** `[Tool_ID: uint8, Command_code: uint8, Value: uint16]`

### 5.2 Commissioning Steps
1. **Frame Squareness & Belt Tensioning:** Verify the two parallel 2040 longitudinal rails are aligned within 0.5 mm diagonal tolerance. Tension GT2 9mm belts until acoustic resonance reaches 75 Hz over a 150 mm span.
2. **TB6600 DIP Switches:** Set current limit to 2.8A peak and microstepping to 1/8 steps (1600 steps/rev).
3. **BLE Provisioning:** Pair the Flutter companion app with the gantry node. Verify manual jog sliders on X and Y axes move in the correct Cartesian directions.
