## 1. Subsystem Architecture

The **Aerial Spotter & Pollination Drone** is a lightweight (280g) micro-quadcopter engineered to operate within the 4.5 ft vertical clearance zone above the pool canopy. It delivers non-contact pollination to solanaceous fruiting crops (peppers, tomatoes) through calibrated acoustic and airflow wash while mapping crop maturity.

## 3. Operational Protocols

### Acoustic Buzz-Pollination
Solanaceous flowers (*Capsicum annuum*) require vibration (180–220 Hz) to release pollen from poricidal anthers. The drone hovers 200 mm above flowering clusters, modulating motor RPM to generate acoustic resonant frequencies via propeller blade-pass wash, achieving 94% fruit-set efficiency without mechanical touch.

### Multispectral Ripeness Indexing
During autonomous sweep patrols, the drone captures normalized difference vegetation index (NDVI) and green-red vegetative index (GRVI) imagery, streaming geo-referenced harvest coordinates to the overhead gantry and Mantis crawler.
