## 3. Modular Canopy Decoupling: Universal Solar Roof Standard (RFC-0009)

In accordance with [RFC-0009: Universal Helical Shutter Solar Roof Standard](/open-biosphere/rfcs/rfc-0009-helical-shutter-solar-roof/), the TPS Root-Zone Chamber decouples subterranean root misting from canopy solar irradiation:
* **Canopy PAR Delivery:** Diffuse, high-intensity natural sunlight is bounced omnidirectionally across the upper vine trellis via the inverted 45° specular pyramid deflector (`PART-OPT-CONE-DIFFUSER-PYRAMID`).
* **Complete Root Darkness:** Because all solar collection and shutter attenuation occur inside the central vertical tower, the lower aeroponic root tank maintains 100% darkness (&lt; 0.05 lux), completely preventing tuber greening and toxic solanine synthesis.
* **Thermal Regulation:** When ambient greenhouse temps spike, the rotary shutter occludes up to 95% of solar infrared gain, protecting delicate potato root hair transpirational health.

## 6. BLE Peripheral Operation & Runbook

### 6.1 BLE Service & Telemetry GATT Map
* **Service UUID:** `0000FFE0-0000-1000-8000-00805F9B34FC`
* **Telemetry Characteristic (`0xFFE1`, Notify):** `[Root_Zone_Temp_C: float32, Relative_Humidity_Pct: float32, Reservoir_Level_mm: uint16, Pressure_PSI: uint16]`
* **Misting Command Characteristic (`0xFFE2`, Write):** `[Pulse_Duration_Sec: uint8, Interval_Minutes: uint16, Force_Cycle: uint8]`

### 6.2 Commissioning & Operating Steps
1. **Chamber Sealing:** Fit pre-slit neoprene collars into the 24 lid ports. Verify that the lid perimeter EPDM gasket compresses evenly when latched, achieving total darkness (less than 0.05 lux).
2. **Manifold Pressurization:** Route the 1/4" nylon pressure lines from the 24V booster pump to the 6 brass misting nozzles mounted at Z = 300 mm. Test at 120 PSI to ensure complete 30-50 µm atomization with zero dripping.
3. **Manual Fallback Connection:** Keep the hand-pump pressure sprayer connected to the parallel bypass ball valve for zero-power emergency operation during grid failure.
4. **BLE Pairing & Calibration:** Pair via BLE GATT using the Open Biosphere companion app. Configure misting interval (default: 5-second pulse every 3 minutes) and monitor root zone temperature.
