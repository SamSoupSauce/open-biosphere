## 3. Tri-Modal Control Architecture

The dosing and environmental array can be actuated by manual gravity valves, physical priming push-buttons, or closed-loop AI telemetry:

| Control Mode | Actuation Mechanism | Operator / System Experience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Manual (Zero Power)** | 3-way L-port ball valves + gravity burettes | Turn physical valve handles to draw nutrients from graduated wall burettes directly into the water stream; adjust brass needle valve on the CO2 regulator by hand. |
| **Tier 2: Buttons & Dials** | 6 individual tactile push-buttons + rate dials | Press dedicated momentary green buttons to prime and dispense each pump channel manually; turn potentiometer knobs to dial in delivery volume without touching software. |
| **Tier 3: Autonomous AI** | 1 Hz closed-loop PID microcontroller loop | Reads real-time pH, EC, temperature, and CO2 electrodes over isolated Modbus RS-485, executing micro-doses automatically with predictive drift compensation. |

## 5. Master Biosphere Assembly Interlock

In the integrated 20x10 ft Open Biosphere installation:

1. **Perimeter Wall Mounting**: Mounts in an IP65 splash-proof enclosure directly beside the South-West datum origin $(0,0,0)$.
2. **Manifold Injection**: Dosing lines inject concentrated stock solutions directly into the fluidic return line after the biofiltration loop, ensuring complete turbulent mixing before water re-enters the Central Basin and NFT gutters.
3. **Master CAN / MQTT Bus**: Publishes environmental telemetry and pump actuation logs to the centralized Open Biosphere logging server.
