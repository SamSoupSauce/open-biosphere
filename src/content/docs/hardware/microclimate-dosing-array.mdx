## 3. Instructions (Base Zero-Power Machine)

The foundational build of the Microclimate & Dosing Array is a **100% mechanical, zero-power gravity-fed chemical dosing backplane**. Utilizing six 250 mL graduated glass/HDPE burettes with PTFE stopcocks, 3-way manual L-port PVC ball valves, a high-precision brass gas needle valve, and colorimetric test kits, it allows off-grid growers to meter concentrated nutrients and balance water pH with clinical precision without electricity.

### Mechanical Architecture & Decoupling Standard
1. **Wall-Mount Dosing Backplane**: An 8 mm pre-drilled chemical-resistant acrylic or HDPE sheet (600 × 450 mm) mounts vertically above the reservoir.
2. **Graduated Wall Burettes**: Six 250 mL transparent graduated cylinders with permanent white enamel 1.0 mL graduations and leak-free PTFE stopcock valves allow measuring exact dose volumes before release.
3. **3-Way L-Port Bypass Valves**: 1/4" manual L-port valves connect the burettes to the nutrient injection manifold. Rotating the handle 90° isolates the line from future peristaltic pumps, allowing pure gravity-fed dosing.
4. **Mechanical Decoupling Standard**: When motorized peristaltic pumps are retrofitted in Add-On 1, the 3-way L-port ball valves remain inline. Flipping a valve handle by 90° instantly bypasses the peristaltic pump rollers, allowing direct gravity drainage from the burettes if power is interrupted.

### Step-by-Step Fabrication & Assembly Protocol

#### Step 1.1: Backplane Drilling & Wall Mounting
1. Cut a 600 mm × 450 mm sheet of 8 mm thick transparent acrylic or white marine HDPE.
2. Drill mounting holes on a standardized 50 mm grid for six burette spring clamps, six 3-way valves, and the gas needle valve.
3. Secure the backplane to the greenhouse frame at eye level ($Z = 1,400$ mm) using four M6 stainless lag screws with rubber dampening washers.

#### Step 1.2: Graduated Burette & PTFE Stopcock Mounting
1. Snap six pairs of rubber-lined stainless steel spring clamps into the upper backplane positions.
2. Insert six 250 mL graduated burettes with 0.1 mL scale divisions into the clamps.
3. Verify that the bottom PTFE stopcock handles rotate smoothly through 360° without binding or chemical weeping.

#### Step 1.3: 3-Way L-Port Bypass Plumbing Installation
1. Mount six 1/4" NPT 3-way L-port PVC ball valves directly below each burette stopcock.
2. Connect each burette outlet to Port 1 (Top) of its corresponding 3-way valve using 1/4" ID food-grade chemical-resistant fluoroelastomer (FKM/Viton) tubing.
3. Plumb Port 2 (Bottom) of all six valves into a common 1/2" CPVC discharge injection manifold.
4. Leave Port 3 (Side) capped with a removable push-to-connect plug, reserved for motorized peristaltic pump integration in Add-On 1.

#### Step 1.4: High-Precision Gas Needle Valve Mounting
1. Mount a high-precision brass micro-metering needle valve to the lower-right corner of the backplane.
2. Plumb 1/4" high-pressure polyurethane tubing from an external $CO_2$ gas regulator (set to 15 PSI delivery pressure) to the needle valve inlet.
3. Plumb the needle valve outlet to a porous ceramic air diffuser stone submerged in the main water basin or air circulation duct.

#### Step 1.5: Chemical Leak & Calibration Verification
1. Fill all six burettes with 200 mL of clean water and close stopcocks. Let stand for 4 hours; verify zero volume loss or joint seepage.
2. Turn stopcocks to open; verify full 200 mL gravity drain empties into the manifold in $< 45$ seconds.

### Step-by-Step Operating Runbook (Manual Mode)

1. **Pre-Dose Water Quality Check**: Take a 50 mL sample from the main reservoir. Using the manual liquid test drop kit, measure pH (colorimetric comparator) and EC (manual battery pen). Note target adjustments (e.g. pH is 7.4; target is 6.5).
2. **Burette Stock Loading**: Open the stock bottle cap. Pour concentrated chemical (e.g. 10% Phosphoric Acid for pH Down) into the top funnel of Burette 5 until liquid reaches the exact calculated dose line (e.g. 18.0 mL).
3. **Volume Verification**: Check the meniscus at eye level against the graduation markings. Close the top dust cap.
4. **3-Way Valve Alignment**: Ensure the corresponding 3-way L-port valve handle points to GRAVITY DRAIN (Port 1 to Port 2 open).
5. **Executing Gravity Discharge**: Rotate the PTFE stopcock 90° open. The measured 18.0 mL dose gravity-feeds through the FKM line directly into the turbulent water return stream, dispersing instantly.
6. **Manifold Rinse**: Pour 30 mL of distilled water through the burette to flush any concentrated acid out of the manifold line. Close stopcock.
7. **Manual $CO_2$ Adjustment**: Inspect the atmospheric $CO_2$ colorimetric test card in the plant canopy. Crack open the brass micro-metering needle valve 1/8th turn until bubble counter reads 2 bubbles/second, then re-check in 2 hours.

## 4. Add-On 1: Semi-Autonomous Electromechanical Pack (BLE Virtual Panel)

Under [RFC-0005](/rfcs/rfc-0005-hardware-interlocks-ble-virtual-panel), physical control panels with dedicated priming buttons, rotary dials, and multi-turn analog potentiometers are strictly banned across all machine tiers to eliminate liquid breach points and control priority contention.

Add-On 1 motorizes chemical delivery governed by the **Strict Two-Control Physical Standard**:
1. **Master Hardware DC Power Switch**: Heavy-duty toggle switch directly isolating the 12V DC power bus.
2. **"Nuke & Pave" SD Factory Restore Button**: Dedicated recessed tactile switch wired to trigger an automatic secondary bootloader re-flash from micro-SD storage.

All channel priming, calibrated pulse volume metering (0.1 to 30.0 mL), and atmospheric $CO_2$ solenoid pulsing are conducted via the **Flutter Companion App Virtual Panel** over direct local BLE GATT.

### Electromechanical Actuation Architecture & Decoupling
- **Peristaltic Dosing Pumps**: Six 12V DC brushed peristaltic pumps with 3-roller rotors and food-grade PharMed BPT / silicone tubing ($0.15$ mL per rotor revolution, 100 mL/min peak).
- **Mechanical Decoupling**: The 3-way L-port valves from the Base build connect to the pump outputs. Rotating the valve handle 90° isolates the peristaltic tubing completely, restoring instant gravity-drain burette operation if power fails.
- **Strict Two-Control Enclosure**: An IP65 waterproof enclosure mounted beside the manifold contains:
  - Heavy-duty toggle switch directly isolating the 12V DC supply line.
  - Recessed tactile button next to the 0.91" I2C OLED display (`[DOSER-A1 | BLE READY]`) for 5-second SD recovery reflash.
  - Micro-SD card slot module pre-loaded with golden recovery `firmware.bin` and `config.default`.
- **Atmospheric Gas Solenoid**: 12V DC direct-acting brass $CO_2$ solenoid valve with a mechanical manual override thumb-pin, allowing gas to be locked on, pulsed electrically, or opened by hand.
- **Ephemeral BLE Virtual Panel**: When nearby, an operator opens the Flutter companion app on mobile device. The app auto-discovers the doser over BLE GATT and displays real-time virtual touch controls: 6-channel momentary virtual prime pads, calibrated pulse volume sliders, and a $CO_2$ solenoid purge toggle.

### Step-by-Step Retrofit Protocol

#### Step 2.1: Peristaltic Pump Mounting
1. Bolt six 12V peristaltic pump heads into the pre-drilled central holes on the acrylic backplane using M3 stainless screws.
2. Plumb the suction tube of each pump directly into concentrated stock gallon jugs resting beneath the panel.
3. Plumb the discharge tube of each pump into Port 3 (Side) of the corresponding 3-way L-port bypass valve.

#### Step 2.2: Two-Control Enclosure & Driver Board Assembly
1. Mount an IP65 sealed ABS junction box on the lower aluminum rail.
2. Install the heavy-duty inline 12V master switch, recessed SD reset tactile button, micro-SD slot module, and 0.91" I2C OLED display.
3. Mount the ULN2803A 8-channel Darlington driver board and ESP32 BLE peripheral controller inside the junction box.
4. Wire driver outputs to the six peristaltic pump motor leads.

#### Step 2.3: 12V $CO_2$ Electronic Solenoid Installation
1. Thread a 12V DC normally-closed brass solenoid valve inline between the gas regulator and the high-precision needle valve.
2. Wire solenoid coil through driver channel 7 with flyback diode protection.
3. Test manual override: press the spring-loaded brass pin on the solenoid body to verify gas flows even with power disconnected.

#### Step 2.4: Power Distribution & BLE Firmware Flashing
1. Mount the 12V 5A MeanWell power supply to the backplane base with an IEC fused power switch.
2. Flash the ESP32 controller with the Doser Virtual Panel peripheral firmware; verify it advertises `[DOSER-A1]`.
3. Verify onboard OLED renders `[DOSER-A1 | BLE READY]`.

#### Step 2.5: Volumetric Calibration Routine via Mobile App
1. Place a 50 mL graduated cylinder under each pump discharge line.
2. Open the Flutter companion app; tap the virtual prime pad for Channel 1 for exactly 10 seconds.
3. Adjust calibration factor in app until 10-second pulse registers exactly $16.6\text{ mL} \pm 0.2\text{ mL}$. Repeat for all 6 channels.

### Step-by-Step BLE Virtual Panel Operating Runbook

1. **Power Initialization**: Flip master 12V DC toggle switch ON. The onboard 0.91" OLED illuminates: `[DOSER-A1 | BLE READY]`. Verify all six peristaltic lines are primed with zero bubbles.
2. **Walk-Up BLE Virtual Panel Connection**: Open the Flutter companion app on mobile device. Select `DOSER-A1` from discovered BLE peripherals. The app instantly renders the 6-channel Dosing Virtual Panel.
3. **Channel Priming**: If an air bubble is spotted in a suction line, tap and hold the corresponding virtual prime button in the app until solid liquid discharges into the manifold.
4. **Setting Calibrated Doses**: Inspect current water EC and pH. Adjust virtual dose sliders in the app (e.g. Channel 1: 5.2 mL of Nitrogen stock; Channel 5: 3.0 mL of pH Down).
5. **Triggering the Dosing Cycle**: Tap the virtual "Execute Dosing Cycle" button. The selected peristaltic pumps run for their calibrated durations, automatically delivering the exact chemical volumes before shutting off.
6. **$CO_2$ Enrichment Toggle**: Toggle the virtual $CO_2$ switch ON in the app. The solenoid opens, releasing $CO_2$ through the pre-set needle valve at a steady 2 bubbles/second.
7. **"Nuke & Pave" SD Recovery Protocol**: If calibration values corrupt or firmware locks up, hold the physical recessed reset button for 5 seconds during power-on. The secondary bootloader reflashes factory baseline firmware from micro-SD in 4 seconds.
8. **Immediate Zero-Power Bypass Override**: If power fails during an outage, turn the 3-way L-port valves 90° to open the burette ports, fill burettes by hand, and resume manual gravity dosing.

## 5. Add-On 2: Agentic Autonomous AI Pack (Edge Vision & Closed Loop)

Under [RFC-0004](/rfcs/rfc-0004-controller-node-tool) and [RFC-0006](/rfcs/rfc-0006-agnostic-controller-runtime), the **Agentic Autonomous AI Pack** transforms the dosing panel into a 24/7 self-regulating **Controller-Node-Tool (CNT)** water chemistry orchestrator. It operates as a dedicated **Doser Node** commanding modular **Edge Tools** over BLE, receiving nutrient recipe ratios and 1 Hz closed-loop PID setpoints from the Central Biosphere Controller over Wi-Fi.

### Autonomous Vision & Telemetry Architecture
- **Doser Machine Node (ESP32-S3)**: Dual-Core Xtensa LX7 (240 MHz, 8 MB PSRAM) mounted inside an IP65 enclosure governed by the **Strict Two-Control Physical Standard** (Master DC Power Switch and Recessed SD Reset Button alongside 0.96" I2C OLED `[NODE: DOSER-01 | LINK -> 4096]`). Uplinks to Central Controller via Wi-Fi; collects telemetry and downlinks to Tools via BLE Central.
- **Galvanically Isolated Chemistry Probes**: Dual-junction sealed lab-grade pH probe and platinum dual-pin EC electrode connected via Atlas Scientific galvanic isolation circuits, eliminating ground-loop electrical noise from submersible pumps.
- **Photoacoustic Ambient Gas Sensor**: Sensirion SCD41 photoacoustic NDIR sensor measuring atmospheric $CO_2$ (400 to 5,000 ppm $\pm 40$ ppm), air temperature ($\pm 0.8^\circ	ext{C}$), and relative humidity ($\pm 2\%$).
- **Dedicated Modular Edge Tools (ESP32-C3)**: Driven 1:1 by micro ESP32-C3 controllers equipped with 0.42" micro OLED displays and **250ms hardware deadman watchdogs**:
  - `DEV_PUMP_BANK1`: Micro ESP32-C3 with 0.42" OLED (`TOOL: PUMP_BANK1 | LINK: OK`) + 250ms watchdog driving peristaltic pumps 1–3 (N, P, K).
  - `DEV_PUMP_BANK2`: Micro ESP32-C3 with 0.42" OLED (`TOOL: PUMP_BANK2 | LINK: OK`) + 250ms watchdog driving peristaltic pumps 4–6 (Micro, pH Down, pH Up).
  - `DEV_WATER_SNS`: Micro ESP32-C3 with 0.42" OLED (`TOOL: WATER_SNS | LINK: OK`) reading isolated pH and EC telemetry.
  - `DEV_GAS_CTL`: Micro ESP32-C3 with 0.42" OLED (`TOOL: GAS_CTL | LINK: OK`) + 250ms watchdog reading Sensirion SCD41 $CO_2$ and pulsing the 12V $CO_2$ solenoid valve.
- **250ms Deadman Watchdog Fail-Safe**: If any Tool loses BLE connection to the Doser Node for $> 250$ ms, it drops immediately into high-Z safe state: all peristaltic pumps halt immediately and the $CO_2$ solenoid snaps closed, preventing catastrophic chemical dumping.

### Step-by-Step Autonomous Multi-Agent Runbook

1. **Continuous 1 Hz Telemetry Polling**: Every second, the Doser Node polls `DEV_WATER_SNS` and `DEV_GAS_CTL`. Data is streamed over Wi-Fi to the Central Controller sliding average filter to eliminate pump electrical noise.
2. **Autonomous Closed-Loop pH Dosing**: If pH drifts above 6.55, the Central Controller calculates required acid volume. It dispatches a micro-pulse command to the Doser Node, which signals `DEV_PUMP_BANK2` (Pump 5: pH Down) for 0.8 mL, followed by a 180-second mixing delay.
