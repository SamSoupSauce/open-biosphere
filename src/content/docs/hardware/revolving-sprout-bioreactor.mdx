The **Revolving Dark-Chamber Sprout Bioreactor** is a modular, standalone agricultural appliance engineered for continuous, rapid vegetative protein generation. While designed to dock into the Perimeter Lower Tier of the master Open Biosphere assembly, it functions as an autonomous benchtop or cabin appliance for homesteaders and off-grid setups.

## 2. Technical Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Footprint Dimensions** | 450 mm (W) × 600 mm (D) × 500 mm (H) |
| **Drum Geometry** | Cylindrical food-grade perforated drum: 300 mm diameter × 450 mm length |
| **Batch Capacity** | 800 g dry seed (yields ~3.5 kg wet hypocotyls per 96-hour run) |
| **Rotation Drive** | NEMA 17 planetary geared stepper motor (5.18:1 reduction) |
| **Rotation Speed** | 0.5 to 2.0 RPM intermittent (rotates 15 seconds every 15 minutes) |
| **Irrigation System** | Dual high-pressure atomizing misting nozzles (50 µm droplet size) |
| **Sanitation Subsystem** | 200 mg/h corona-discharge ozone generator with gas diffuser stone |
| **Power Consumption** | Average 18W continuous (12V/24V DC dual rail, peak 45W during misting) |
| **Controller Architecture** | ESP32-S3 microcontroller with WiFi/BLE telemetry & local web UI |

## 4. Master Biosphere Assembly Interlock

When deployed within the full Open Biosphere enclosure (~20x10 ft footprint):

1. **Mechanical Docking**: The drum chassis features a standardized V-slot bottom dovetail rail that locks into the Perimeter Lower Tier mounting brackets ($Z = 150$ mm above water line).
2. **Autonomous AGV Exchange**: The Ground Track AGV pod carrier uses an articulated pneumatic clamp to grip the drum's central hub, unlatching and shuttling spent canisters to the harvest station.
3. **Plumbing Quick-Connect**: Magnetic-seat fluid disconnects tap directly into the enclosure's ozone sanitizing loop and nutrient drain manifold.
4. **Communications Bus**: Communicates via CAN bus / ESPHome MQTT to the centralized master biosphere orchestrator.
