## 3. Tri-Modal Control Architecture

The sprout bioreactor can be operated completely off-grid by hand, controlled with simple push-buttons, or driven autonomously by AI orchestration:

| Control Mode | Actuation Mechanism | Operator / System Experience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Manual (Zero Power)** | Hand crank + mechanical ratchet pawl + gravity drain valve | Rotate the drum manually 5 turns twice a day using the external hand crank; open the brass ball valve by hand to drain rinse water without any electrical connection. |
| **Tier 2: Buttons & Dials** | Tactile push-button + analog dial timer | Press the green panel button to jog the drum to any position; turn the physical rotary timer dial to set misting frequency; flip a toggle switch to run an ozonated wash cycle. |
| **Tier 3: Autonomous AI** | Edge microcontroller schedule + AGV telemetry | ESP32-S3 executes precise 96-hour growth program, dynamically modulating rotation and misting based on ambient humidity; signals Ground Track AGV when harvest is ready. |

## 5. Master Biosphere Assembly Interlock

When deployed within the full Open Biosphere enclosure (~20x10 ft footprint):

1. **Mechanical Docking**: The drum chassis features a standardized V-slot bottom dovetail rail that locks into the Perimeter Lower Tier mounting brackets ($Z = 150$ mm above water line).
2. **Autonomous AGV Exchange**: The Ground Track AGV pod carrier uses an articulated pneumatic clamp to grip the drum's central hub, unlatching and shuttling spent canisters to the harvest station.
3. **Plumbing Quick-Connect**: Magnetic-seat fluid disconnects tap directly into the enclosure's ozone sanitizing loop and nutrient drain manifold.
4. **Communications Bus**: Communicates via CAN bus / ESPHome MQTT to the centralized master biosphere orchestrator.
