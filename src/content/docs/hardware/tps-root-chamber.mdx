The **TPS Aeroponic Root-Zone Chamber** is a high-yield, disease-free mini-tuber production appliance. Engineered to eliminate soil pathogens and heavy substrate handling, it can be constructed as an independent homestead seed-potato propagator or integrated into the Central Basin of the master Open Biosphere assembly.

---

## 1. Standalone Homesteading & Off-Grid Utility

Traditional seed potatoes require disease-prone clonal tubers that degrade over generations and demand large soil footprints. This machine enables homesteaders to germinate **True Potato Seed (TPS)**—tiny botanical seeds weighing 1/1000th of a tuber—and produce clean, pathogen-free mini-tubers continuously in a compact footprint:

## 2. Technical Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Chassis Dimensions** | 1,200 mm (L) × 800 mm (W) × 650 mm (H) |
| **Planting Density** | 12 to 18 TPS root collars per square meter |
| **Misting Cycle** | 5 seconds pulse every 3 minutes (closed-loop timer) |
| **Droplet Size** | 30 to 50 µm ultrasonic atomization |
| **Operating Pressure** | 80 to 110 PSI high-pressure line or piezoelectric disc array |
| **Light Ingress** | Less than 0.05 lux (tested via EPDM foam labyrinth seals) |
| **Harvest Yield** | 40 to 60 mini-tubers (15–35g each) per plant per cycle |
| **Controller Architecture** | ESP32-C3 with redundant pressure transducers and liquid level floats |

## 4. Master Biosphere Assembly Interlock

In the integrated 20x10 ft Open Biosphere installation:

1. **Sub-Surface Basin Suspension**: The chamber docks into the southern bay of the Central Basin, suspended over the nutrient reservoir.
2. **Overhead Gantry Toolhead**: The 2-Axis Overhead Gantry descends through the top light-seal bellows with a soft-silicone suction toolhead, harvesting mature mini-tubers non-destructively.
3. **Plumbing Integration**: Drains directly into the primary MBBR biofiltration sump, creating a continuous gravity return.
