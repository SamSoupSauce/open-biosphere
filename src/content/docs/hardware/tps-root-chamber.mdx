## 3. Tri-Modal Control Architecture

The root-zone chamber supports manual pressure-wand hydration, mechanical dial timers, or automated ultrasonic misting:

| Control Mode | Actuation Mechanism | Operator / System Experience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Manual (Zero Power)** | Manual hand-pump sprayer + sliding inspection flap | Pressurize nutrient solution in an off-grid garden pump sprayer; insert wand through light-sealed silicone flap to mist roots by hand; reach into baffled hatch to pick tubers. |
| **Tier 2: Buttons & Dials** | Heavy-duty toggle switch + mechanical dial timer | Flip rocker switch to run booster pump; twist spring-wound countdown timer to execute a 15-minute misting session without microcontrollers or code. |
| **Tier 3: Autonomous AI** | Ultrasonic pulse sequencing + gantry coordination | ESP32-C3 pulses 50 µm mist every 180 seconds based on root transpiration sensors; signals overhead gantry to descend and harvest mature mini-tubers non-destructively. |

## 5. Master Biosphere Assembly Interlock

In the integrated 20x10 ft Open Biosphere installation:

1. **Sub-Surface Basin Suspension**: The chamber docks into the southern bay of the Central Basin, suspended over the nutrient reservoir.
2. **Overhead Gantry Toolhead**: The 2-Axis Overhead Gantry descends through the top light-seal bellows with a soft-silicone suction toolhead, harvesting mature mini-tubers non-destructively.
3. **Plumbing Integration**: Drains directly into the primary MBBR biofiltration sump, creating a continuous gravity return.
