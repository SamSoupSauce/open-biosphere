The **Open Biosphere** is an umbrella project for an open-source hardware registry of modular agricultural machines. 

Every machine detailed below is designed around a single guiding principle: **to enable end-users, makers, and homesteaders to build essential agricultural and off-grid machines out of common, easily ordered commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) parts and 3D prints**.

Each machine functions as a standalone tool—whether on a kitchen counter, inside a hoop house, or over a farm pond—and all machines interlock into the master ~20x10 ft pool biosphere.
