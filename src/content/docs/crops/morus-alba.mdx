## System Alignment & Biology

Dwarf bush mulberries (*Morus alba* cv. 'Issai') are trained as two-dimensional espaliers against the Trellised Espalier wall ($Z = 2.5$ to $4.0$ ft). They produce abundant dark purple-black syncarpous berries continuously over a 60-day window.

---

## Why It Fits ML Tooling & Robotics

- **Mantis Branch-Crawler Interface**: Rigid espalier wires allow the dual-silicone roller robot to climb and navigate limbs directly above the fruit canopy.
- **Stereo Color Ripeness Classification**: RGB-NIR stereo cameras distinguish mature black berries from firm green/red immature fruit.
- **Vacuum Cushioned Catch Pod**: Berries are sheared at the pedicel and drawn into a foam-lined vacuum pod, preventing fruit bruising.

---

## Bioactive & Nutritional Payoff

- **1-Deoxynojirimycin (1-DNJ)**: 120 mg/100g dw. Natural alpha-glucosidase inhibitor that stabilizes postprandial blood sugar.
- **Anthocyanins & Resveratrol**: 280 mg/100g dw. Potent vascular antioxidants.

---

## Culinary Applications

- Eaten fresh, simmered into berry compotes, or dehydrated into shelf-stable dark fruit snacks.
