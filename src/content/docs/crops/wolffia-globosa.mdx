## System Alignment & Biology

*Wolffia globosa* is the smallest flowering plant on Earth, reproducing vegetatively via budding every 30 to 36 hours under optimal lighting. In the closed biosphere pool, it occupies **70% of the upper open-water surface area (110 sq ft)** within the Central Basin.

Its unique morphology completely bypasses complex robotic end-effectors, pollination mechanisms, or structural pruning. It thrives under closed-loop surface skimming, narrow-band dynamic LED dosing, and fluidic nutrient replenishment.

---

## Why It Fits ML Tooling & Robotics

- **Non-Contact Volume Estimation**: Continuous surface-area pixel tracking via top-down RGB/NIR vision allows exact biomass volume estimation without physical contact.
- **Rapid Reinforcement Learning Loops**: Sub-48-hour doubling times provide RL agents with immediate feedback on photoperiod adjustments, PAR variations, and N:P:K nutrient dosing.
- **Zero Structural Waste**: No non-edible roots, stems, or woody fiber. Harvest requires only fluid surface skimming through wedge-wire filters.
- **Dynamic Nitrogen Tuning**: Highly responsive to nitrate spikes to adjust crude protein fractions up to 45% dry weight.

---

## Nutritional Density & Human Palatability

- **Complete Protein**: 40%–45% dry weight protein with an essential amino acid profile rivaling whole egg protein (rich in leucine, lysine, and branched-chain amino acids).
- **Essential Lipids**: High ratio of Alpha-Linolenic Acid (ALA Omega-3) to Omega-6 fatty acids.
- **Micronutrient Bank**: Highly bioavailable Vitamin B12, iron (28.5 mg/100g), zinc, and antioxidant carotenoids (lutein/zeaxanthin).

## Closed-Loop Integration

Floating *Wolffia* mats act as the biosphere's primary biological biofilter. As nutrient solution from perimeter aeroponic gutters drains into the central pool, *Wolffia* actively strips dissolved nitrates and phosphorus, purifying water before it re-enters the primary sump and high-pressure root misting manifolds.

## Culinary Applications

Neutral, mild vegetal flavor with no astringency or bitterness.
- **Fresh**: Consumed immediately as a nutrient-dense green paste or blended into shakes.
- **Processed**: Flash-dehydrated into high-protein green flour, extruded into pasta, or baked into emergency biosphere ration wafers.
