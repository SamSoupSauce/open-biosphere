## System Alignment & Biology

To maximize fresh food volume without occupying valuable photon airspace, mung beans (*Vigna radiata*) are germinated in light-tight revolving canisters mounted under the perimeter deck.

---

## Why It Fits ML Tooling & Robotics

- **Zero-Photon Production**: Operates in total darkness, recycling nutrient drainage water via 4-hour automatic rinse cycles.
- **AGV Dock & Exchange**: Ground rovers dock with the canisters on a 4-to-6-day cycle, transporting mature drums directly to automated separation stations.
- **Optical Sprout Lidar**: Measures hypocotyl elongation through quartz inspection windows.

---

## Bioactive & Nutritional Payoff

- **Rapid Turnaround**: 4 days from dry seed to mature succulent sprout.
- **Vitexin & GABA**: Rich in C-glycosylflavonoids and GABA (140 mg/100g dw) synthesized during dark anaerobic germination.

---

## Culinary Applications

- Exceptionally crisp, juicy, and refreshing. Eaten raw in salads, stir-fried with garlic, or added to soups.
