## System Alignment & Biology

*Monostroma nitidum* is a delicate green macroalga (*Aosa*) grown in an isolated marine tumble-culture sump alongside the Central Basin. It serves as a benchmark negative-control anchor for saline robotics corrosion and tumble-culture vision tracking.

---

## Why It Fits ML Tooling & Robotics

- **Turbidity & Spectral Attenuation**: Measured via submerged dual-wavelength light sensors rather than surface RGB cameras.
- **Centrifugal Dewatering**: Continuous harvest via rotating stainless wedge-wire vortex separation drums.

---

## Nutritional & Bioactive Payoff

- **Rhamnan Sulfate**: 6,200 mg/100g dw. Highly sulfated marine polysaccharide that protects the vascular endothelial glycocalyx and demonstrates antithrombotic properties.
- **Bioavailable Algal B12 & Iodine**: 6.5 µg cobalamin and 2,400 µg marine iodine per 100g.

---

## Culinary Applications

- Dried into emerald flakes and dusted over rice bowls (*aonori*), or steeped into savory dashi broths.
