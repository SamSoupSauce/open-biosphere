## System Alignment & Biology

*Physalis peruviana* (Cape Gooseberry or Goldenberry) is a bushy perennial solanaceous plant that produces bright orange berries encased in papery lantern-like calyces (*husks*). It is trained along the Trellised Espalier guideframes against the perimeter wall.

---

## Why It Fits ML Tooling & Robotics

- **NIR Calyx Penetration**: Multi-spectral NIR backlighting penetrates the papery husk to assess internal berry pigmentation and diameter without tearing the husk.
- **Passive Drop Catchment / Soft Gripper**: Harvested either through vibration funnels into lower catchment bins or via soft silicone micro-grippers.

---

## Bioactive & Nutritional Payoff

- **Physalins (Withanolides)**: 145 mg/100g dw. Potent steroidal lactones that inhibit NF-kB activation and suppress systemic pro-inflammatory cytokines.
- **Carotenoids & Vitamin C**: Abundant in beta-carotene, beta-cryptoxanthin, and Vitamin C (28 mg/100g).

---

## Culinary Applications

Intensely aromatic sweet-tart flavor with notes of pineapple, mango, and tomato.
- Eaten fresh as a dessert fruit, dehydrated into golden raisins, or simmered into savory camp glazes.
