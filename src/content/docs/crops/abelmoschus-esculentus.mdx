## System Alignment & Biology

Dwarf hydroponic okra cultivars (such as 'Baby Bubba') are grown in aeroponic perimeter troughs under high-intensity LED lighting. The plant produces daily cream-colored flowers that set pods within 24 hours.

---

## Why It Fits ML Tooling & Robotics

- **Daily Elongation Derivative**: Pods grow 2–4 cm per 24 hours. 2D computer vision calculates the growth curve derivative to trigger harvesting at tender 6.5–8.5 cm lengths before lignification occurs.
- **Micro-Bypass Shears**: High-speed shears clip the pedicel 5 mm above the pod cap, avoiding trichome dust and foliage abrasion.

---

## Nutritional & Mucilaginous Payoff

- **Galacturonic Acid / Pectin Complex**: 3,400 mg/100g dw. Viscous soluble prebiotic fibers that bind intestinal bile acids and blunt glycemic spikes.
- **Bioavailable Calcium & Vitamin K**: 82 mg calcium (low oxalate, highly absorbed) and 31.3 µg Vitamin K1.

---

## Culinary Applications

- Blistered whole in cast-iron skillets.
- Sliced into broths as an all-natural culinary thickening agent.
