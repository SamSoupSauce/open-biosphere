## System Alignment & Biology

Dwarf bush peppers are grown in aeroponic troughs along the Perimeter Mid Tier under focused high-intensity light spectra.

---

## Why It Fits ML Tooling & Robotics

- **Acoustic Drone Pollination**: Micro-quadcopters hover above flowering plants, using directed propeller downwash (1.5–2.5 m/s) to deliver resonant acoustic vibration (buzz pollination) without physical flower contact.
- **Color Metric Ripeness Tracking**: Hyperspectral cameras track the chlorophyll degradation to carotenoid transition to trigger pedicel harvesting.

---

## Bioactive & Nutritional Payoff

- **Ultra-High Vitamin C**: 143.7 mg per 100g fresh pepper (&gt; 160% Daily Value).
- **Capsaicin**: 350 mg/100g dw. Stimulates metabolic thermogenesis, promotes endorphin release, and acts as a natural antimicrobial agent.

---

## Culinary Applications

- Roasted over flame, diced into salsas, or dried into fiery paprika spice flakes.
