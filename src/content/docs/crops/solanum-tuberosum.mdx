## System Alignment & Biology

To satisfy baseline caloric requirements without occupying massive soil acreage, the biosphere utilizes True Potato Seed (TPS) genetics cultivated in specialized subterranean aeroponic root misting sumps suspended over the Central Basin.

---

## Why It Fits ML Tooling & Robotics

- **Acoustic & Sub-Surface Thermal Imaging**: High-frequency acoustic sensors evaluate tuber diameter within the dark root chamber (target harvest size: 30–45 mm).
- **Pneumatic Selective Harvester**: Soft suction-cup end-effectors detach mature mini-tubers individually without tearing delicate stolons, allowing continuous harvesting rather than destructive whole-plant termination.
- **Micro-Misting Feedback**: Ultrasonic 30–50 µm mist nozzles pulse every 3–5 minutes based on root-zone humidity and transpiration sensors.

---

## Nutritional & Caloric Payoff

- **Staple Carbohydrates**: 87 kcal and 20.1g complex carbohydrates per 100g.
- **Resistant Starch (Type 3)**: Formed after gentle cooking and cooling, delivering essential short-chain fatty acids (butyrate) to support intestinal barrier integrity.
- **Electrolytes**: High potassium (421 mg/100g) and Vitamin C.

---

## Culinary Applications

Tender, waxy mini-tubers.
- Steamed whole with olive oil and fresh rosemary.
- Roasted in skillets or mashed into staple carbohydrate rations.
