## System Alignment & Biology

Compact dwarf snap bean cultivars are grown in sub-irrigated grow trays positioned along the low-profile Perimeter Lower Tier service corridor. Their roots fix atmospheric nitrogen via symbiotic *Rhizobium* colonies, reducing synthetic nitrate dosing.

---

## Why It Fits ML Tooling & Robotics

- **AGV Stereo-Depth Geometry Mapping**: Pod geometry is recognized against identical green background foliage using stereo depth disparity maps.
- **2-Finger Silicone Clamp & Shears**: Ground rovers clamp the pod pedicel and execute a clean cut without tearing the main stem.

## Nutritional & Botanical Payoff

- **Dietary Fiber & Plant Protein**: 2.7g fiber and 1.83g protein per 100g fresh pods.
- **Bioavailable Silicon**: 8.5 mg/100g dw. Essential for collagen synthesis and bone mineral structure.

## Culinary Applications

- Steamed fresh with sea salt, sautéed in garlic oil, or quick-pickled in cider brine.
