## System Alignment & Biology

True wasabi (*Wasabia japonica*) is the benchmark for micromanagement-dependent crops. In nature, it demands pristine, cold, highly oxygenated mountain spring water flowing over gravel gravel beds (*sawa* method).

In the pool enclosure, it is cultivated in dedicated shaded gravel channels along the perimeter tier, fed by chilled, highly aerated recirculating water (DO > 8.0 mg/L).

---

## Why It Fits ML Tooling & Robotics

- **Closed-Loop Chilling Control**: Extreme sensitivity to root-zone thermal drift makes it ideal for automated RL loop control (water target: 10°C–13°C).
- **High Chemical & Economic Density**: High yields of isothiocyanates (allyl isothiocyanate and 6-MSITC) provide potent antimicrobial and circulatory health benefits per square foot.
- **Early Fungal Anomaly Detection**: Because wasabi takes 15 to 18 months to reach maturity, multi-spectral computer vision monitors petioles continuously to detect *Pythium* or *Rhizoctonia* symptoms before root damage occurs.

---

## Nutritional & Bioactive Payoff

- **Allyl Isothiocyanate (AITC)**: 1,200 mg/100g dw. Activates TRPA1 ion channels, clearing respiratory passages and killing foodborne bacteria.
- **6-MSITC**: 450 mg/100g dw. Induces Nrf2 antioxidant enzymes, inhibits platelet aggregation, and improves cerebral blood flow.

---

## Culinary Utilization

Signature sharp, clean sinus heat that dissipates quickly without oily lingering burning.
- Freshly grated on sharkskin (*oroshigane*) or micro-ceramic graters immediately prior to consumption.
- Petioles and leaves are pickled in brine to produce crunchy *wasabi-zuke*.
