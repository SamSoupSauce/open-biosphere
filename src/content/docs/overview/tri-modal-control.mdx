A core vulnerability of modern agricultural technology and robotics is **brittle complexity**. When a system requires high-speed internet, cloud servers, complex software stacks, and microcontrollers just to turn a valve or harvest a tray, a blown fuse, software bug, or off-grid blackout turns expensive equipment into useless scrap.

Conversely, hardwiring physical button panels, rotary dials, and analog potentiometers across a high-humidity greenhouse leads to rapid contact corrosion, water ingress, and priority contention ("control fights") between manual switches and autonomous firmware.

The **Open Biosphere Tri-Modal Control Standard** (enforcing [RFC-0004](/rfcs/rfc-0004-controller-node-tool) and [RFC-0005](/rfcs/rfc-0005-hardware-interlocks-ble-virtual-panel)) mandates that every machine in the ecosystem must be operable across three distinct, parallel tiers of control:

## 1. The Three Operating Tiers

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Tier 1: 100% Mechanical Manual Fallback" icon="hands">
    **Zero electrical power required.** In an off-grid battery failure, emergency shutdown, or EMP event, an operator can operate the machine entirely by hand using physical levers, manual valves, hand cranks, and mechanical slip-clutches.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tier 2: Ephemeral BLE Virtual Panel (Strict Two-Control Hardware)" icon="setting">
    **Zero physical button corrosion or control contention.** Under [RFC-0005](/rfcs/rfc-0005-hardware-interlocks-ble-virtual-panel), physical jog panels, dials, and potentiometers are banned. Physical hardware features strictly **two controls**: (1) True Inline Master DC Power Disconnect, and (2) SD Factory Restore Tactile Button. Manual jogging, pump priming, and speed adjustments run via the Flutter companion app over local BLE.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tier 3: Autonomous AI & Agent Orchestration" icon="cpu">
    **Fully autonomous closed-loop intelligence.** Under [RFC-0004](/rfcs/rfc-0004-controller-node-tool) and [RFC-0006](/rfcs/rfc-0006-agnostic-controller-runtime), the system operates on a Controller-Node-Tool (CNT) model: a portable Central Controller runtime (`biosphere-core` running on any Linux/macOS host) orchestrates machine Nodes (ESP32-S3) over an isolated `POTFRM-<CLUSTER_ID>` Wi-Fi AP (`192.168.42.0/24`), while Nodes coordinate dedicated edge Tools (ESP32-C3) over BLE with 250ms hardware deadman watchdogs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Universal Mechanical Decoupling" icon="lock">
    Manual controls never fight electronic actuators. Hand levers utilize override clutches, dual-yoke linkages, or parallel bypass plumbing, allowing seamless transition between manual and automated modes.
  </Card>

## 2. Mechanical Decoupling & Hardware Interlock Mechanisms

To ensure manual inputs, motorized servos, and emergency procedures operate without stripping gears or fighting motor detent torque, designs employ three standard patterns:

### Pattern A: Dual-Yoke & Slip-Clutch Linkages (For Levers & Rotary Motion)
- **Application**: Gantry axes, crawler clamps, and mechanical skimmer weirs.
- **Mechanism**: The servo or stepper drives the actuation shaft through a spring-loaded friction slip-clutch or slotted bell crank. A manual hand lever or crank is pinned directly to the output shaft.
- **Operation**: Moving the manual hand lever slips the clutch smoothly with zero damage to the motor gearbox. When electronic control resumes, the motor drives the shaft normally.

### Pattern B: Parallel Manual Bypass Plumbing (For Valves & Pumps)
- **Application**: Fluidic skimmer flow, nutrient dosing injection, and aeroponic misting loops.
- **Mechanism**: Standard 3-way L-port ball valves or tee-junction bypass lines are installed in parallel with electronic solenoid and peristaltic pumps.
- **Operation**: 
  - In **Auto/BLE Mode**, the manual valve is left in the "Auto" position, routing flow through the motorized pumps and electronic solenoids.
  - In **Manual Mode**, opening the physical ball valve lever allows direct gravity drainage, siphon action, or manual hand-pump pressurization.

### Pattern C: Strict Two-Control Hardware Interlock (RFC-0005)
- **Application**: All machine nodes and edge tool enclosures.
- **Control 1 (True Hardware Power Disconnect)**: Heavy-duty inline mechanical switch on the positive DC feed line. Instantly de-energizes the board and all attached actuators for maintenance with zero quiescent sleep leakage.
- **Control 2 (SD Factory Restore Button)**: Recessed tactile button next to the status OLED. Holding for 5 seconds on boot triggers the secondary bootloader, which reflashes the golden `firmware.bin` and `config.default` from the onboard micro-SD slot module, wiping NVS partitions and restoring clean factory baseline.
