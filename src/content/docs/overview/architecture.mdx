This specification defines the physical enclosure blueprint, multi-agent drone routing, and automated command-line auditing pipelines for an enclosed **~6,000-gallon pool biosystem** (~20x10 ft footprint, 4.5 ft vertical clearance).

All candidate plant species, nutritional breakdowns, and ML evaluations (-1.0 to 10.0 scale) remain completely isolated in dedicated database contracts to prevent clutter and enable machine parsing.

---

## 1. Physical Envelope & Enclosure Zoning (~200 sq ft Footprint)

The enclosure occupies a standard swimming pool basin retrofitted with tiered vertical aeroponics and hydroponic gutters:

| Zone Identifier | Spatial Boundary | Target Crops & Subsystems | Operational Mechanisms |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Central Basin** | 160 sq ft, 4.5 ft liquid depth | Non-vascular duckweed, *Wolffia*, True Potato Seed (TPS) mini-tubers | Primary skimming channel, 30–50μm aeroponic root misting, particulate dewatering, bio-filter loop |
| **Perimeter Lower Tier** | 40 sq ft (0.5 – 1.2 ft elevation) | Sub-irrigated bush beans, unlit sprout bioreactors | Ground rover harvesting access, rotating dark hypocotyl canister docking bays |
| **Perimeter Mid Tier** | 40 sq ft (1.2 – 2.5 ft elevation) | Basal rosettes (sorrel, purslane), dwarf sweet & chili peppers | Hydroponic NFT gutters, automated nutrient balance control |
| **Trellised Espalier** | 40 sq ft (2.5 – 4.0 ft vertical wall) | Dwarf bush mulberries, goldenberry vines | Rigid vertical wire guideframes for branch-crawling robot indexing |

### Aerial Spotter & Pollination Drones
- Micro-quadcopters utilizing calibrated propeller wash (1.5–2.5 m/s) to deliver non-contact acoustic and airflow vibration pollination for fruiting peppers.
- Downward-facing multi-spectral RGB/NIR sensors detect fruit ripeness indices and generate targeted harvest coordinates.

### Branch-Crawling 'Mantis' Robot
- Dual-actuated soft silicone pinch rollers engineered to navigate rigid horizontal and angled limbs without slippage.
- High-precision micro-shears paired with vacuum-assisted collection pods enable soft fruit pedicel harvesting without mechanical bruising.

### Ground Track AGVs (Automated Guided Vehicles)
- Low-profile tracked units operating within designated perimeter service corridors.
- Fitted with automated mechanical docking arms to exchange revolving unlit sprout canisters on a 4-to-6-day cycle.
- Articulated 3-axis stereo-depth arms perform selective lateral pod severing for ground-level yields.

## 3. Closed-Loop Nutrient Recycling Kinetics

The fluidic loop is designed around nitrogen cascading:
1. **Bio-Digestion**: Organic waste and plant trimmings are anaerobically digested into bioavailable ammonium and nitrate ions.
2. **High-EC Consumers**: The concentrated effluent is first routed to perimeter NFT channels (purslane, peppers, wasabi) requiring EC 1.8–2.4 mS/cm.
3. **Runoff Filtration**: Drain water flows down into the central basin where floating mats of *Wolffia globosa* scavenge residual nitrates and phosphorus, operating as a biological polishing filter (target EC 1.2–1.5 mS/cm).
4. **Aeroponic Sumps**: Polished water is pressurized through 30–50μm misters directly onto suspended True Potato Seed (TPS) root systems before returning to the main sump.
