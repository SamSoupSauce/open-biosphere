**Open Biosphere** is an umbrella open-hardware project and research platform for automated closed agriculture. Rather than a single monolithic apparatus, it is designed as a **suite of smaller, modular machines** built from common or easily ordered parts (COTS).

Each machine fulfills an autonomous homesteading, farming, or off-grid function on its own—and all machines interlock seamlessly into a **Master Biosphere Assembly** within a standardized ~20x10 ft footprint (~200 sq ft, 4.5 ft vertical clearance, ~6,000-gallon pool envelope).

## 2. Physical Envelope & Spatial Enclosure (The Master Assembly)

When assembled together, the individual machines occupy four interlocking spatial tiers within a standard ~20x10 ft retrofitted pool envelope:

| Zone Identifier | Spatial Boundary | Interlocking Machines & Target Crops | Operational Mechanisms |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Central Basin** | 160 sq ft, 4.5 ft liquid depth | **Fluidic Skimmer Loop**, **TPS Aeroponic Chambers**; *Wolffia*, potatoes, *Monostroma* | Continuous surface skimming, solids dewatering, moving-bed biofiltration, sub-surface ultrasonic root misting |
| **Perimeter Lower Tier** | 40 sq ft (0.5 – 1.2 ft elevation) | **Ground Track AGV**, **Revolving Sprout Bioreactors**; bush beans, mung hypocotyls | Ground rover navigation, magnetic floor tape, automated canister unlatching and shuttling |
| **Perimeter Mid Tier** | 40 sq ft (1.2 – 2.5 ft elevation) | **Aerial Spotter Drone Dock**, **Microclimate Dosing Array**; purslane, peppers, wasabi | Recirculating hydroponic NFT gutters, prop-wash acoustic buzz pollination, precision nutrient/pH dosing |
| **Trellised Espalier** | 40 sq ft (2.5 – 4.0 ft vertical wall) | **Mantis Branch-Crawler Robot**; dwarf mulberries, goldenberries | Rigid stainless steel wire guideframes, silicone pinch-roller climbing, cushioned vacuum fruit harvesting |

## 4. Closed-Loop Nutrient Recycling Kinetics

The fluidic loop is designed around nitrogen cascading between machines:
1. **Bio-Digestion**: Organic waste and spent root matter are digested into bioavailable ammonium and nitrate ions.
2. **High-EC Consumers**: The concentrated effluent is first routed through perimeter NFT gutters (purslane, peppers, wasabi) requiring EC 1.8–2.4 mS/cm.
3. **Biological Polishing Filter**: Drain runoff cascades into the central basin where floating *Wolffia globosa* scavenges residual nitrates and phosphorus, operating as a rapid biological filter (target EC 1.2–1.5 mS/cm).
4. **Aeroponic Root Sumps**: Polished water is pressurized through 30–50μm misters directly onto suspended True Potato Seed (TPS) root systems before returning to the main sump.
