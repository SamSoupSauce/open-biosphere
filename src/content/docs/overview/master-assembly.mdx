The **Open Biosphere** is an umbrella open-hardware project. Rather than requiring builders to construct a single monolithic, multi-thousand-dollar apparatus, the entire system is architected as an **interlocking ecosystem of smaller, autonomous, standalone machines**.

Each machine is built from accessible, commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) components, 3D printed brackets, and standard aluminum extrusions. Builders can start with a single machine for their homestead, cabin, or greenhouse, and progressively combine them into the **Master Biosphere Assembly** within a standard ~20x10 ft pool envelope.

## 2. Core Integration Standards

To ensure each machine functions seamlessly both as a standalone appliance and as a docked component in the master assembly, all designs adhere to four universal interfaces:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="1. Standard Spatial Datum (0,0,0)" icon="compass">
    The South-West corner of the pool coping at water level ($Z = 0$) serves as the master Cartesian origin $(0,0,0)$ in millimeters. Every gantry coordinate, drone AprilTag, and AGV docking bay is indexed to this absolute reference.
  </Card>
  <Card title="2. Unified 24V DC Power Backbone" icon="zap">
    All stationary machines and docking stations run on a standard 24V DC bus, simplifying solar/battery off-grid integration and eliminating hazardous high-voltage AC wiring over open water.
  </Card>
  <Card title="3. Standardized Extrusion Mounts" icon="grid">
    Perimeter structural extrusions (4040 and 2040 V-slot aluminum) provide universal mechanical rails. Machines clip or bolt in with standard M5 drop-in T-nuts and 3D printed PETG brackets.
  </Card>
  <Card title="4. Isolated Communications Bus" icon="laptop">
    Subsystems exchange telemetry and coordination triggers over an isolated RS-485 Modbus and CAN bus network, bridging to an open-source ESPHome / Home Assistant / ROS2 orchestrator.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## 3. Physical Enclosure Assembly Walkthrough

### Phase 1: Perimeter Structural Rail Installation
1. Fasten **4040 V-slot aluminum extrusions** around the coping of the ~20x10 ft pool basin (6,096 mm × 3,048 mm).
2. Align the South-West corner bracket to establish the absolute **$(0,0,0)$ Spatial Datum**.
3. Affix high-contrast **AprilTag (36h11 family) visual fiducials** along the top extrusion faces at 1,000 mm intervals for autonomous drone localization.
4. Mount the **Overhead Gantry Y-axis guide rails** on the upper extrusion lip at elevation $Z = 1,200$ mm.

### Phase 2: Fluidic Loops & Plumbing Manifold
1. Position the **Fluidic Skimmer & Dewatering Separator** on the southern coping channel ($Y = 600$ mm).
2. Install the 1.5-inch PVC bottom return line running from the skimmer's Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR) down to the bottom sump ($Z = -1,372$ mm).
3. Connect the **Microclimate & Automated Dosing Array** injection manifold into the filtered return stream to ensure complete chemical mixing before water enters the grow tiers.
4. Seat the **TPS Aeroponic Root Chambers** into the southern bay suspended over the water line.

### Phase 3: Perimeter Tiers & Robotic Transit Lanes
1. Fasten the **Perimeter Lower Tier** brackets ($Z = 150$ to $365$ mm above water level) and lay the magnetic induction track tape for the **Ground Track AGV**.
2. Mount the **Revolving Sprout Bioreactor** docking cradles along the lower tier maintenance corridor.
3. Install the **Perimeter Mid Tier** hydroponic NFT channels ($Z = 365$ to $760$ mm) directly above the AGV lane.
4. String stainless steel espalier guideframe wires (150 mm vertical pitch) along the North-East vertical boundary wall ($Z = 760$ to $1,220$ mm) for the **Mantis Branch-Crawler**.

### Phase 4: Compute & Network Commissioning
1. Mount the master controller cabinet (Raspberry Pi CM4 + CAN bus transceiver) adjacent to the South-West origin.
2. Power up the 24V DC MeanWell power supplies and verify voltage stability across all perimeter tap points.
3. Verify telemetry heartbeats from each machine's ESP32 node via the local web dashboard.
