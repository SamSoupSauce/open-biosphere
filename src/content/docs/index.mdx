## Umbrella Architecture Pillars

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Umbrella Open-Hardware Project" icon="seti:graphql">
    A modular suite of **accessible, standalone agricultural appliances** built from globally available COTS parts, 3D prints, and standard aluminum extrusions for homestead and off-grid food resilience.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tri-Modal Control Standard" icon="setting">
    Every machine is operable across three tiers: **100% by hand with mechanical valves & levers**, **with servos & physical push buttons**, or **autonomously with edge AI vision & closed-loop PID**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Master Biosphere Assembly" icon="puzzle">
    Each machine operates on its own or interlocks into the **~20x10 ft footprint (~200 sq ft, 4.5 ft vertical clearance)** master pool enclosure with **~6,000 gallons** of closed-loop recirculating nutrients.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Continuous ML Data Contracts" icon="open-book">
    Rigorous JSON Schema contracts assessing computer vision volume tracking, automated end-effector feasibility, and complete micronutrient/bioactive density.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
