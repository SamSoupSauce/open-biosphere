# [RFC-0009 / OPT-001] Universal Helical Shutter Solar Roof & Passive Thermal Collector Standard

## 1. Executive Summary & Design Law
Direct solar exposure introduces uncontrolled thermal spikes, solar scorching, and catastrophic pest/blight vectors through ventilation gaps. Conversely, full-artificial indoor lighting imposes heavy electrical operating costs and fragile dangling fixtures.

RFC-0009 establishes the **Universal Helical Shutter Solar Roof** as the canonical standard roof assembly for all Open Biosphere growth chambers requiring solar PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation). The system decouples solar collection from environmental exposure by pairing a passive, keyed external mirror spoke array with an internal, single-actuator **Helical Rotary Pipe Shutter**.

This establishes a modular architectural boundary: the Solar Roof serves as a universal optical utility, allowing underlying growth chambers (aquatic flumes, aeroponic root zones, soil beds) to vary in dimensions and mechanisms independently.

---

## 2. Zero-Plastic Material Constraint
In accordance with Open Biosphere biological purity standards, all components of the roof assembly must strictly adhere to the non-leaching palette:
* **Structural Chassis & Spokes:** Stamped 5052/6061 aluminum or 304 stainless steel sheet.
* **Optical Surfaces:** Flat back-silvered float glass mirrors (no acrylic/polycarbonate).
* **Aperture Windows:** High-transmission borosilicate flat glass discs.
* **Rotary Sleeves & Shafts:** Seamless drawn aluminum tubing.
* **Seals & Gaskets:** High-temperature vulcanized EPDM or food-grade silicone rubber.
* **Zero Plastics:** Absolute ban on PVC, POM/Delrin, acrylic, nylon, or 3D-printed polymers in the optical or thermal path.

### Subsystem A: The Keyed Spoke Array (Static Exterior)
* **Error-Proof Assembly:** The outer rim features radial spoke brackets laser-cut with indexed slots and numbered positions (`SPOKE-01` to `SPOKE-16`).
* **Fixed Geometry:** Mirror mounting tabs have factory-bent angle detents. The builder places mirror panel #1 into slot #1, panel #2 into slot #2. Zero manual optical alignment, zero laser calibration tools, and zero field adjustments required.
* **Weatherproof Passive Operation:** Zero wiring, motors, or moving parts reside outdoors.

### Subsystem B: The Helical Pipe Shutter (Rotary Valve)
* **Concentric Dual-Tube Assembly:**
  * **Outer Cylinder (Static):** Acts as the hermetic structural barrier. Borosilicate glass discs are set into portholes ascending in a 360° helical spiral up the shaft, each aligned with a specific external mirror facet.
  * **Inner Cylinder (Rotary Shutter):** A precision-fit concentric aluminum sleeve with matching spiral window cutouts.
* **Single-Actuator Control:** A single 12V/24V metal-gear servo or NEMA-17 stepper motor mounted at the base of the central column rotates the inner sleeve through a 0°–90° sweep:
  * **$0^\circ$ (Full Bore / 100% PAR):** All inner cutouts align perfectly with the outer glass portholes. Maximum solar flux enters the core.
  * **$0^\circ \rightarrow 90^\circ$ (Progressive Attenuation):** Rotating the sleeve progressively occludes the portholes from bottom to top, acting like a cylindrical mechanical iris.
  * **$90^\circ$ (Thermal Dump / Standby):** The solid aluminum pipe wall covers all portholes. Over 95% of direct solar radiation is rejected, protecting the chamber from overheating during high-temperature midday peaks.

### Subsystem C: Down-Core Dispersion & Ceiling Dome
* The regulated light beam travels down the central bore onto an inverted, polished multi-faceted aluminum pyramid deflector.
* The pyramid throws the light horizontally onto the chamber's perimeter, which reflects upward onto a smooth, matte-white dome ceiling.
* The dome scatters the concentrated rays into an even, diffuse, non-burning photon shower across the growing surface.
* **Hybrid Light Port:** A single high-efficiency supplemental LED puck is recessed into the apex of the central deflector. During overcast conditions or night cycles, the LED projects along the identical optical diffusion path, eliminating dangling fixtures over the crop canopy.

## 4. Control Interface & Node Integration

In accordance with the Controller-Node-Tool (CNT) standard (RFC-0004) and Minimalist Hardware Interlocks (RFC-0005):

* **Tool ID:** `TOOL-OPT-SHUTTER-01`
* **Node Connection:** Plugs directly into the chamber's standard ESP32 Node via a 3-pin PWM/stepper header.
* **Tri-Modal Control:**
  1. **Manual Mode:** Mechanical thumb-screw collar at the base allows manual rotation and locking of the shutter without electrical power.
  2. **Virtual Panel (BLE):** The companion app provides a real-time slider (`0% - 100% Shutter Aperture`) for manual commissioning and calibration.
  3. **Autonomous Closed-Loop:** The local Node monitors an internal ambient PAR sensor (e.g., AS7262 / BH1750) and target crop microclimate tables, continuously adjusting the servo angle to maintain target daily light integral (DLI) while capping thermal rise.
